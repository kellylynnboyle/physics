# Unit 1: Kinematics — Missed-Question Quiz

Name: ______________________  Period: ______  Date: ____________

Each question targets a mistake students commonly make. Use g = 9.8 m/s² and ignore air resistance unless told otherwise.

**1.** A ball is thrown straight up. At the very top of its path, what is its acceleration?

- A. Zero, because it has stopped
- B. 9.8 m/s² downward
- C. 9.8 m/s² upward
- D. It depends on how hard it was thrown

**2.** An object is moving in the negative direction and has a negative acceleration. What is it doing?

- A. Slowing down
- B. Speeding up
- C. Moving at constant speed
- D. Turning around

**3.** A runner completes one full lap of a 400 m track in 100 s. What is her average velocity?

- A. 4 m/s
- B. 0.25 m/s
- C. 0 m/s
- D. 40 000 m/s

**4.** On a position-time graph, a straight line slopes upward. What is the object doing?

- A. Speeding up
- B. Moving at constant velocity
- C. Sitting at rest
- D. Moving uphill

**5.** On a velocity-time graph, a horizontal line sits at +5 m/s. What is the object doing?

- A. At rest
- B. Moving at a constant 5 m/s
- C. Accelerating at 5 m/s²
- D. Returning to the start

**6.** A bowling ball and a tennis ball are dropped from the same height at the same time. Ignore air resistance. Which lands first?

- A. The bowling ball, because it is heavier
- B. The tennis ball, because it is lighter
- C. They land at the same time
- D. It cannot be predicted

**7.** A car starts from rest with constant acceleration. Compared with the distance it covers in the first 2 s, how far does it travel in the first 4 s?

- A. The same distance
- B. Twice as far
- C. Three times as far
- D. Four times as far

**8.** What does the area under a velocity-time graph represent?

- A. Acceleration
- B. Displacement
- C. Average speed
- D. Time

**9.** A car goes around a curve at a steady 20 m/s. Is it accelerating?

- A. No, its speed is constant
- B. Yes, because its direction is changing
- C. Only if the driver presses the gas
- D. Only if the road is banked

**10.** A rock is dropped from rest. Ignoring air resistance, how fast is it moving after 2.0 s? (g = 9.8 m/s²)

- A. 4.9 m/s
- B. 9.8 m/s
- C. 19.6 m/s
- D. 39.2 m/s
