# Practice Problems: Kinematics

Use g = 9.8 m/s², ignore air resistance. Show: diagram, knowns/unknown with units, equation, algebra, answer with units, reasonableness check. Answer key at the bottom.

## Set A: Concepts (K1, K2, K3)

1. A runner jogs 400 m around a track and ends where she started in 100 s. Find her average speed and average velocity.
2. A car has v = +20 m/s and a = −3 m/s². Is it speeding up or slowing down? What if v = −20 m/s and a = −3 m/s²?
3. Sketch an x-t graph for (a) an object at rest, (b) constant positive velocity, (c) speeding up in the positive direction, (d) moving back toward the origin at constant speed.
4. A v-t graph rises linearly from 0 to 8 m/s in 4 s, stays at 8 m/s for 3 s. Find the acceleration in the first part and the total displacement.

## Set B: Calculation (K4)

5. A sprinter runs 100 m in 12.5 s at constant velocity. Find the velocity.
6. A car accelerates from rest to 25 m/s in 8.0 s. Find a and the distance covered.
7. A car moving at 30 m/s brakes at −6.0 m/s². Find the stopping distance and stopping time.
8. A train moving at 10 m/s accelerates at 0.50 m/s² for 20 s. Find its final velocity and displacement.

## Set C: Free fall (K5)

9. A rock is dropped from a bridge and hits the water after 3.0 s. Find the bridge height and the impact speed.
10. A ball is thrown straight up at 15 m/s. Find (a) max height, (b) total time in the air, (c) velocity after 2.0 s.
11. A ball dropped from rest falls for 1 s, then 2 s total. How much farther does it fall during the second second than during the first?

## Set D: Challenge (extending)

12. A car accelerates from rest at 2.0 m/s² for 5.0 s, then cruises at constant velocity for 10 s. Find the total displacement and sketch the v-t and x-t graphs.
13. Two stones: one dropped from a 20 m cliff, the other thrown downward at 5 m/s from the same cliff. How much sooner does the thrown stone land?
14. Explain, using the v² = v₀² + 2aΔx equation, why a car going 60 mph needs about four times the braking distance of a car going 30 mph.

## Answer key

1. Average speed = 400 m / 100 s = **4 m/s**. Average velocity = 0 / 100 s = **0**.
2. Slowing down (v + and a −: opposite signs). Second case: v − and a − (same sign) → **speeding up** in the negative direction.
3. (a) horizontal line, (b) straight line, positive slope, (c) concave-up curve with increasing slope, (d) straight line, negative slope.
4. a = 8/4 = **2 m/s²**. Displacement = ½(4)(8) + (3)(8) = 16 + 24 = **40 m**.
5. v = 100/12.5 = **8.0 m/s**.
6. a = 25/8 = **3.1 m/s²**. Δx = ½(0 + 25)(8) = **100 m**.
7. Δx = (0 − 900)/(2 × −6) = **75 m**. t = (0 − 30)/(−6) = **5.0 s**.
8. v = 10 + 0.5(20) = **20 m/s**. Δx = 10(20) + ½(0.5)(400) = 200 + 100 = **300 m**.
9. h = ½(9.8)(9.0) = **44 m**. v = 9.8(3.0) = **29 m/s** (downward).
10. (a) h = 15²/(2 × 9.8) = **11.5 m**. (b) t = 2(15)/9.8 = **3.1 s**. (c) v = 15 − 9.8(2.0) = **−4.6 m/s** (4.6 m/s downward).
11. First second: 4.9 m. Total after 2 s: 19.6 m. Second second: 14.7 m. So **9.8 m farther** (3× as far).
12. Phase 1: v = 10 m/s, Δx₁ = ½(2)(25) = 25 m. Phase 2: Δx₂ = 10 × 10 = 100 m. Total **125 m**. v-t: ramp up to 10 m/s then flat; x-t: parabola then straight line.
13. Dropped: t = √(2 × 20/9.8) = 2.02 s. Thrown: 20 = 5t + 4.9t² → 4.9t² + 5t − 20 = 0 → t = [−5 + √(25 + 392)]/9.8 = (−5 + 20.42)/9.8 = 1.57 s. About **0.45 s sooner**.
14. Δx = v₀²/(2|a|). Doubling v₀ quadruples v₀², so for the same braking acceleration, the stopping distance is **4×** (and reaction distance, covered at constant speed, is 2×).
