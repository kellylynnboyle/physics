# Unit 2: Projectile Motion — Missed-Question Quiz

Name: ______________________  Period: ______  Date: ____________

Each question targets a mistake students commonly make. Use g = 9.8 m/s² and ignore air resistance unless told otherwise.

**1.** A bullet is fired horizontally from a rifle. At the same instant, a second bullet is dropped from the same height. Ignore air resistance. Which hits the level ground first?

- A. The dropped bullet
- B. The fired bullet
- C. They hit at the same time
- D. It depends on the fired bullet's speed

**2.** A ball is kicked at an angle. At the highest point of its path, what is its velocity?

- A. Zero
- B. Equal to its launch velocity
- C. Horizontal, equal to vx
- D. Straight down at 9.8 m/s

**3.** At the highest point of a projectile's path, what is its acceleration?

- A. Zero
- B. 9.8 m/s² downward
- C. 9.8 m/s² upward
- D. 9.8 m/s² in the direction of motion

**4.** Ignoring air resistance, what happens to a projectile's horizontal velocity during flight?

- A. It decreases steadily
- B. It increases steadily
- C. It stays constant
- D. It is zero at the top

**5.** A plane flying level at constant velocity drops a package. Ignoring air resistance, where is the plane when the package hits the ground?

- A. Far ahead of the package
- B. Directly above the package
- C. Behind the package
- D. It depends on the mass of the package

**6.** After a baseball leaves the pitcher's hand, which forces act on it? (Ignore air resistance.)

- A. Gravity only
- B. Gravity and the force of the throw
- C. The force of the throw only
- D. No forces act on it

**7.** A marble rolls off a table at speed v and lands a distance x from the table. If it rolls off at 2v, how long is it in the air compared with before?

- A. Half as long
- B. The same time
- C. Twice as long
- D. Four times as long

**8.** Two balls are launched at the same speed on level ground, one at 30° and one at 60°. How do their ranges compare?

- A. The 30° ball goes farther
- B. The 60° ball goes farther
- C. They land at the same distance
- D. Neither lands

**9.** A ball rolls horizontally off a 4.9 m high ledge at 5.0 m/s. How far from the base does it land? (g = 9.8 m/s²)

- A. 2.5 m
- B. 4.9 m
- C. 5.0 m
- D. 24.5 m

**10.** A ball is launched at 20 m/s at 30° above the horizontal. What is the vertical component of its velocity?

- A. 10 m/s
- B. 17.3 m/s
- C. 20 m/s
- D. 30 m/s
