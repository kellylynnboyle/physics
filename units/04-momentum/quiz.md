# Unit 4: Momentum — Missed-Question Quiz

Name: ______________________  Period: ______  Date: ____________

Each question targets a mistake students commonly make. Use g = 9.8 m/s² and ignore air resistance unless told otherwise.

**1.** A heavy truck and a small car collide head-on. How do the sizes of their changes in momentum compare?

- A. The truck's change is larger
- B. The car's change is larger
- C. They are equal
- D. Neither changes momentum

**2.** A rubber ball and a lump of clay with the same mass hit a wall at the same speed. The ball bounces back; the clay sticks. Which receives the larger impulse?

- A. The clay
- B. The ball
- C. They receive the same impulse
- D. Neither receives an impulse

**3.** How does an airbag reduce injury in a crash?

- A. It reduces the passenger's change in momentum
- B. It reduces the impulse on the passenger
- C. It increases the stopping time, which reduces the force
- D. It increases the force to stop the passenger sooner

**4.** Two carts collide and stick together. Which statement is correct?

- A. Momentum and kinetic energy are both conserved
- B. Momentum is conserved; kinetic energy is not
- C. Kinetic energy is conserved; momentum is not
- D. Neither is conserved

**5.** A 2 kg cart moving at 3 m/s collides with a 1 kg cart at rest. They stick together. What is their speed afterward?

- A. 1 m/s
- B. 1.5 m/s
- C. 2 m/s
- D. 3 m/s

**6.** Two ice skaters stand still, then push off each other. What is their total momentum afterward?

- A. Zero
- B. Equal to the heavier skater's momentum
- C. Twice the lighter skater's momentum
- D. It depends on how hard they push

**7.** A 0.5 kg ball hits a wall at 4 m/s and rebounds at 4 m/s. What is the size of its change in momentum?

- A. 0 kg·m/s
- B. 2 kg·m/s
- C. 4 kg·m/s
- D. 8 kg·m/s

**8.** A 10 N net force acts on a 5 kg cart, initially at rest, for 3 s. What is the cart's final speed?

- A. 2 m/s
- B. 6 m/s
- C. 15 m/s
- D. 30 m/s

**9.** A cannon fires a cannonball. Compared with the cannonball, the recoiling cannon has:

- A. Less momentum and less speed
- B. The same size momentum and less speed
- C. The same size momentum and the same speed
- D. More momentum and less speed

**10.** You catch a raw egg by letting your hands move backward with it. Why is the egg less likely to break?

- A. Its change in momentum is smaller
- B. The stopping time is longer, so the force is smaller
- C. Its mass is effectively smaller
- D. The impulse is smaller
