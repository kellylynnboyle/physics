## Cornell notes

| Cue / Questions | Notes |
|---|---|
| Distance vs. displacement? | Distance = total path length (scalar). Displacement Δx = x_f − x_i (vector, straight line start→end, with direction). |
| Speed vs. velocity? | Speed = distance/time (scalar). Velocity v = Δx/Δt (vector). Average velocity uses displacement. |
| What is acceleration? | a = Δv/Δt, units m/s². A vector. Velocity and acceleration same sign → speeding up; opposite signs → slowing down. |
| Reading x–t graphs | Slope = velocity. Straight line = constant v; curve = accelerating; flat = at rest. |
| Reading v–t graphs | Slope = acceleration. **Area under curve = displacement.** Crossing the axis = direction reverses. |
| Constant-acceleration equations | v = v₀ + at · Δx = v₀t + ½at² · v² = v₀² + 2aΔx · Δx = ½(v₀ + v)t. Pick the one missing the unknown you don't need. |
| Free fall | Ignoring air resistance all objects accelerate at g = 9.8 m/s² (≈10) downward, regardless of mass. At the top, v = 0 but a is still 9.8 m/s² down. |
| Problem-solving routine | List knowns/unknowns with signs → choose equation → solve algebraically → substitute with units → sanity-check. |

## Practice problems
**Set A – Concepts**
1. A runner completes one lap of a 400 m track in 80 s. Find average speed and average velocity.
2. Sketch v–t graphs for: (a) speeding up in +x, (b) slowing down in +x, (c) speeding up in −x.
3. A ball is thrown straight up. State v and a at launch, top, and just before landing.

**Set B – Calculations**
4. A car goes 0→27 m/s in 6.0 s. Find a and the distance covered. (4.5 m/s², 81 m)
5. A driver traveling 20 m/s brakes at −5.0 m/s². Stopping distance and time? (40 m, 4.0 s)
6. A stone is dropped from a 44 m bridge. Time to reach water and impact speed? (≈3.0 s, ≈29 m/s)

**Set C – Challenge**
7. Car A at constant 25 m/s passes car B starting from rest with a = 3.0 m/s². When and where does B catch A? (≈16.7 s, ≈417 m)
8. Given a v–t graph with three segments, produce the matching x–t graph.
