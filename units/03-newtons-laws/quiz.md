# Unit 3: Newton's Laws — Missed-Question Quiz

Name: ______________________  Period: ______  Date: ____________

Each question targets a mistake students commonly make. Use g = 9.8 m/s² and ignore air resistance unless told otherwise.

**1.** A hockey puck slides across frictionless ice at constant velocity. What is the net force on it?

- A. A forward force that keeps it moving
- B. Zero
- C. A force equal to its weight
- D. A small backward force

**2.** A large truck collides head-on with a small car. During the collision, how do the forces compare?

- A. The truck exerts a larger force on the car
- B. The car exerts a larger force on the truck
- C. The forces are equal in size
- D. Only the truck exerts a force

**3.** A book rests on a table. Earth pulls down on the book. What is the reaction force to this pull?

- A. The table pushing up on the book
- B. The book pushing down on the table
- C. The book pulling up on Earth
- D. There is no reaction force

**4.** An elevator moves upward at constant speed. How does the cable tension compare with the elevator's weight?

- A. Tension is greater
- B. Tension is equal
- C. Tension is smaller
- D. Tension is zero

**5.** A ball is thrown straight up. While it is rising after leaving the hand, which forces act on it? (Ignore air resistance.)

- A. Gravity only
- B. Gravity and a shrinking upward force from the throw
- C. An upward force only
- D. No forces until it starts to fall

**6.** An astronaut travels from Earth to the Moon. What happens to the astronaut's mass and weight?

- A. Both decrease
- B. Mass stays the same; weight decreases
- C. Mass decreases; weight stays the same
- D. Both stay the same

**7.** You push a box across a level floor at constant speed with a horizontal force of 50 N. How large is the friction force?

- A. Less than 50 N
- B. Exactly 50 N
- C. More than 50 N
- D. Zero

**8.** The net force on a cart is doubled and its mass is also doubled. What happens to its acceleration?

- A. It is four times as large
- B. It doubles
- C. It stays the same
- D. It is half as large

**9.** A 2.0 kg object has forces of 14 N to the right and 4 N to the left acting on it. What is its acceleration?

- A. 2 m/s² right
- B. 5 m/s² right
- C. 7 m/s² right
- D. 9 m/s² right

**10.** A horse pulls a cart forward, and the cart pulls back on the horse with an equal force. Why can the cart still accelerate?

- A. The horse's force is slightly larger
- B. The two forces act on different objects, so they do not cancel
- C. The reaction force is delayed
- D. It cannot accelerate
