# Unit 5: Work & Energy — Missed-Question Quiz

Name: ______________________  Period: ______  Date: ____________

Each question targets a mistake students commonly make. Use g = 9.8 m/s² and ignore air resistance unless told otherwise.

**1.** A weightlifter holds a 1000 N barbell motionless above her head for 5 s. How much work does she do on the barbell during that time?

- A. 5000 J
- B. 1000 J
- C. 200 J
- D. 0 J

**2.** A car's speed doubles. What happens to its kinetic energy?

- A. It stays the same
- B. It doubles
- C. It triples
- D. It becomes four times as large

**3.** Two identical balls start from rest at the same height. One is dropped straight down; the other slides down a frictionless ramp. How do their speeds at the bottom compare?

- A. The dropped ball is faster
- B. The ramp ball is faster
- C. The speeds are the same
- D. It depends on the ramp's angle

**4.** A car traveling at 30 m/s needs how much braking distance compared with the same car at 15 m/s? (Same braking force.)

- A. The same distance
- B. Twice the distance
- C. Four times the distance
- D. Half the distance

**5.** You carry a backpack across a level hallway at constant speed. How much work does your upward supporting force do on the backpack?

- A. Zero
- B. Weight × distance walked
- C. Mass × distance walked
- D. It depends on walking speed

**6.** A heavy ball and a light ball are dropped from the same height. Ignoring air resistance, just before landing:

- A. They have the same speed and the same kinetic energy
- B. They have the same speed; the heavy ball has more kinetic energy
- C. The heavy ball is faster and has more kinetic energy
- D. The light ball is faster

**7.** A box slides across a rough floor and stops. What happened to its kinetic energy?

- A. It was destroyed
- B. It became thermal energy in the box and floor
- C. It became potential energy
- D. It is still kinetic energy

**8.** Two students of equal weight climb the same staircase. Ana takes 5 s and Ben takes 10 s. Which statement is correct?

- A. Ana does more work
- B. Ben does more work
- C. They do the same work; Ana has more power
- D. They do the same work and have the same power

**9.** At what point in its swing does a pendulum have the greatest kinetic energy?

- A. At the highest point on either side
- B. At the lowest point
- C. Halfway up
- D. It is the same everywhere

**10.** A 2.0 kg ball is dropped from rest from a height of 5.0 m. Ignoring air resistance, how fast is it moving just before it lands? (g = 9.8 m/s²)

- A. 4.9 m/s
- B. 9.9 m/s
- C. 49 m/s
- D. 98 m/s
