**NGSS link:** HS-PS2-1 (Newton's 2nd law applied in two dimensions). **Quiz:** `quiz/index.html` → Projectile Motion.

## Key concepts
Vector components (sin/cos) · independence of x and y · constant vₓ · vertical free fall · symmetry of trajectories · range vs. angle · effect of air resistance (qualitative).

## Cornell notes

| Cue / Questions | Notes |
|---|---|
| What is a projectile? | Object moving only under gravity after launch. Path is a parabola (ignoring air resistance). |
| Components | vₓ = v cosθ, v_y = v sinθ. Treat x and y separately; time t links them. |
| Horizontal motion | No horizontal force → aₓ = 0 → vₓ constant: x = vₓt. |
| Vertical motion | a_y = −9.8 m/s². y = y₀ + v₀y t − ½gt²; v_y = v₀y − gt. |
| Horizontal launch | v₀y = 0. Fall time t = √(2h/g) — same as simply dropping the object. Range = vₓt. |
| At the peak | v_y = 0, vₓ unchanged, **a = 9.8 m/s² down**. Speed is minimum (= vₓ). |
| Angled launch (same height) | Time of flight = 2v₀y/g. Max height = v₀y²/2g. Range = v² sin2θ / g. |
| Angle & range | Max range at 45°; complementary angles (30°/60°) give equal range; larger angle → higher, longer flight. |
| Air resistance | Real range is shorter and optimum angle is below 45°; we ignore it in the model. |

## Practice problems
**Set A – Concepts**
1. A ball rolls off a table at 2 m/s while another is dropped. Which lands first? Which has greater impact speed?
2. Sketch vₓ–t and v_y–t graphs for a 45° launch.

**Set B – Calculations**
3. A ball leaves a 19.6 m cliff horizontally at 15 m/s. Time of fall, range? (2.0 s, 30 m)
4. A kicked ball: 40 m/s at 30°. Find v₀x, v₀y, time of flight, range. (34.6 m/s, 20 m/s, 4.1 s, ≈141 m)
5. A water balloon is thrown at 12 m/s at 60°. Max height? (≈5.5 m)

**Set C – Challenge**
6. A stunt car leaves a horizontal ramp and must land 10 m away on ground 2.0 m below the ramp. What minimum launch speed is needed? (≈15.6 m/s)
7. Two angles, 25° and ___° give the same range. Explain with sin2θ.

## Real-world applications
Basketball/soccer/golf shots · long jump and javelin · fire hoses and fountains · artillery and rocketry (with drag) · stunt jumps · motion-capture of athletes · water-gun design.
