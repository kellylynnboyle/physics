Light applies the wave model from Unit 8 specifically to electromagnetic
radiation — and to the technologies (solar cells, medical imaging,
communications) and health questions (radiation exposure) built on top of
it.

## Key concepts to master

- **The electromagnetic spectrum** — radio, microwave, infrared, visible,
  ultraviolet, X-ray, gamma ray, ordered by wavelength/frequency/photon
  energy.
- **Photon energy** — higher frequency EM radiation carries more energy
  per photon (`E = hf`), which is why UV/X-ray/gamma radiation can damage
  living tissue in ways radio waves cannot.
- **Digital vs. analog transmission** — how information is encoded onto
  waves, and the tradeoffs (reliability, storage, security) of digital
  encoding specifically.
- **Evaluating scientific claims in media** — assessing whether published
  claims about EM radiation's effects on matter/health are accurate,
  including checking for bias or misrepresented evidence.
- **Technology applications** — how specific devices (solar cells, medical
  imaging, fiber optics, wireless communication) exploit wave behavior to
  transmit information or capture energy.

## Vocabulary checklist

electromagnetic spectrum · photon · photon energy · ionizing radiation ·
digital signal · analog signal · bandwidth (conceptual)

## Learning objectives

By the end of this unit, students will be able to:

1. Order the regions of the EM spectrum by wavelength, frequency, and photon energy, and explain the direct relationship between frequency and photon energy.
2. Evaluate the advantages (and tradeoffs) of digital transmission and storage of information compared to analog.
3. Evaluate the validity and reliability of claims in published materials about the effects of different EM frequencies absorbed by matter (e.g., "5G radiation" claims, UV/sunscreen claims).
4. Explain how a specific technology (solar cell, MRI, fiber-optic cable, etc.) uses wave behavior/interaction with matter to transmit information or capture energy.

## NGSS alignment

**HS-PS4-2.** Evaluate questions about the advantages of using digital
transmission and storage of information.

**HS-PS4-4.** Evaluate the validity and reliability of claims in published
materials about the effects that different frequencies of electromagnetic
radiation have when absorbed by matter.

**HS-PS4-5\*.** Communicate technical information about how some
technological devices use the principles of wave behavior and wave
interactions with matter to transmit and capture information and energy.
Full text: [`../NGSS-ALIGNMENT.md`](../NGSS-ALIGNMENT.md).

**Suggested lab/project:** research and present one EM-spectrum-based
technology (solar cell, MRI, fiber optics, microwave oven, etc.),
explaining the specific wave-matter interaction it relies on — directly
targets HS-PS4-5.
