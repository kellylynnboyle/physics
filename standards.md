# Standards Alignment (CA NGSS, HS Physical Science)

Verify wording at https://www.cde.ca.gov/pd/ca/sc/ngssstandards.asp

| PE | Summary | Units |
|---|---|---|
| HS-PS2-1 | Analyze data to support the claim that Newton's second law describes the mathematical relationship among net force, mass, and acceleration | 1, 2, 3 |
| HS-PS2-2 | Use mathematical representations to support the claim that total momentum of a system is conserved when there is no net external force | 4 |
| HS-PS2-3 | Apply science and engineering ideas to design a device that minimizes the force on a macroscopic object during a collision | 4 |
| HS-PS3-1 | Create a computational model to calculate the change in energy of one component of a system when the change in energy of the other components and the energy flows are known | 5 |
| HS-PS3-2 | Develop and use models to illustrate that energy at the macroscopic scale can be accounted for as a combination of energy associated with motions of particles and energy associated with the relative positions of particles | 5 |
| HS-PS3-3 | Design, build, and refine a device that works within given constraints to convert one form of energy into another | 5 |

Kinematics and projectile motion are the descriptive foundation for HS-PS2-1 (acceleration data from motion). Gravitational/electric field PEs (HS-PS2-4, HS-PS3-5) and the semester 2 topics are covered in the second-semester project.

## Science & Engineering Practices emphasized
Analyzing and interpreting data (graphs, motion sensors); using mathematics and computational thinking; constructing explanations; designing solutions (egg-drop, roller coaster).
