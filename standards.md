# Standards Alignment (California NGSS, HS Physical Science)

Source: <https://www.cde.ca.gov/pd/ca/sc/ngssstandards.asp>. Wording below is paraphrased; use the CDE text for official documents.

## Performance expectations used in Semester 1

| PE | Paraphrased expectation | Units |
|---|---|---|
| HS-PS2-1 | Analyze data to support the claim that Newton's second law describes the mathematical relationship among net force, mass, and acceleration. | 1, 2, 3 |
| HS-PS2-2 | Use mathematical representations to support the claim that total momentum of a system of objects is conserved when there is no net external force. | 4 |
| HS-PS2-3 | Design, evaluate, and refine a device that minimizes the force on an object during a collision. | 4 |
| HS-PS3-1 | Create a computational model to calculate the change in energy of one component of a system when the change in energy of the other components and the energy flows are known. | 5 |
| HS-PS3-2 | Develop and use models to illustrate that energy at the macroscopic scale can be accounted for as motion of particles or energy stored in fields. | 5 |
| HS-PS3-3 | Design, build, and refine a device that converts one form of energy into another within given constraints. | 5 |

(Semester 2 will add HS-PS2-4/5/6 and HS-PS3-5 for fields/electricity/magnetism, HS-PS4 for waves, HS-PS1-8 for nuclear, and HS-ESS2 / HS-ESS1 / HS-ESS3 for plate tectonics and earthquakes.)

## Science & Engineering Practices emphasized
Analyzing and interpreting data (graphs), using mathematics and computational thinking, planning and carrying out investigations, constructing explanations, designing solutions.

## Crosscutting concepts emphasized
Cause and effect; systems and system models; energy and matter (conservation); patterns; scale, proportion, quantity.

## Course learning goals (student-friendly)
1. I can describe and graph motion using displacement, velocity, and acceleration.
2. I can predict the motion of a projectile by treating x and y independently.
3. I can use Newton's laws and free-body diagrams to explain and predict how forces change motion.
4. I can use impulse and conservation of momentum to analyze collisions and design safer devices.
5. I can use work and conservation of energy to track energy transfers and transformations.
6. I can collect, graph, and interpret lab data, and communicate claims with evidence and reasoning (CER).
