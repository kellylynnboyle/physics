# NGSS Alignment (California Physics & Earth Science model, Semester 1)

> **Verify before distributing.** The CDE page (cde.ca.gov/pd/ca/sc/ngssstandards.asp) could not be fetched when this project was generated, so the wording below is paraphrased from the published NGSS HS Physical Science performance expectations. Check against the CDE site and your course's adopted scope.

| PE | Paraphrase | Units |
|----|-----------|-------|
| HS-PS2-1 | Analyze data to support the claim that Newton's second law relates net force, mass, and acceleration | 1, 2, 3 |
| HS-PS2-2 | Use math representations to support the claim that total momentum of a system is conserved when there is no net external force | 4 |
| HS-PS2-3 | Design, evaluate, and refine a device that minimizes the force on a macroscopic object during a collision | 4 |
| HS-PS3-1 | Create a computational model to calculate the change in energy of one component of a system when the others and energy flows are known | 5 |
| HS-PS3-2 | Develop and use models to show energy at the macroscopic scale as a combination of motion and position (stored) energy | 5 |
| HS-PS3-3 | Design, build, and refine a device that converts one form of energy into another | 5 |

Kinematics and projectile motion are the descriptive foundation for HS-PS2-1 (you must describe motion before relating it to force).

## Science & engineering practices emphasized
Analyzing/interpreting data (graphs), using mathematics and computational thinking, constructing explanations, designing solutions, planning investigations.

## Crosscutting concepts
Cause and effect; systems and system models; energy and matter (conservation); patterns.
