# 11th Grade Physics: Semester 1 Learning Project

A shareable project outline for students and instructors covering **Kinematics, Projectile Motion, Newton's Laws, Momentum, and Work and Energy**. The learning goals are the California NGSS performance expectations for the high school *Physics in the Universe* course model (see [STANDARDS.md](STANDARDS.md)).

Second semester (electricity, magnetism, waves, light, plate tectonics, earthquakes, nuclear physics) is mapped to its standards in STANDARDS.md but not yet built out.

## Student checklist (repeat for each unit)

- [ ] Read the unit page and check off the key concepts as they are covered in class
- [ ] Fill in the Cornell notes and write your own summary
- [ ] Self-test: cover the Notes column and answer every cue aloud; mark the ones you miss
- [ ] Complete the practice problems, then check the answers
- [ ] Complete the unit lab and lab report
- [ ] Self-test again a few days later
- [ ] Score 7 of 8 or better on the unit quiz; retry the missed questions until you can explain each one
- [ ] Record your objective scores in the progress tracker

## Instructor checklist

- [ ] Confirm the performance expectations in STANDARDS.md against the CDE page (they could not be verified online when this was generated)
- [ ] Adjust pacing to the school calendar (19 weeks are allotted here)
- [ ] Post the unit page and Cornell notes at the start of each unit
- [ ] Use the quiz as a pre-test to find misconceptions and again as a post-test
- [ ] Score objectives with the 1 to 4 proficiency scale and allow reassessment
- [ ] Run the two design projects: collision device (HS-PS2-3) and energy conversion device (HS-PS3-3)

## Semester at a glance

| Unit | Big idea | Assessed NGSS PEs | Summative evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kinematics | Motion can be described and predicted with graphs and equations | Supports HS-PS2-1 | Unit test, motion graph lab |
| Projectile Motion | Horizontal and vertical motions are independent | Supports HS-PS2-1, HS-PS2-4 | Unit test, landing-point prediction lab |
| Newton's Laws | Net force causes acceleration; forces come in pairs | HS-PS2-1 | Unit test, second-law lab report |
| Momentum | Momentum is conserved; time reduces force | HS-PS2-2, HS-PS2-3 | Unit test, egg drop design report |
| Work and Energy | Energy is conserved and transferred | HS-PS3-1, HS-PS3-2, HS-PS3-3 | Unit test, computational model, design project |

## How progress is tracked

1. **Objectives**: each unit lists five or six numbered objectives (K1 to K6, P1 to P5, N1 to N6, M1 to M6, E1 to E6).
2. **Evidence**: every objective names the task that shows it (exit ticket, lab, problem set, quiz question, test).
3. **Scale**: each objective is scored 1 to 4; 3 is proficient.
4. **Mastery**: all objectives at 3 or 4 and a quiz score of 7 of 8 or better.
5. **Reassessment**: students correct their work and retry.
