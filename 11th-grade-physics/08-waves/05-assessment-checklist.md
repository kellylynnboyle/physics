# Unit 8: Waves — Assessment & Mastery Checklist

## Instructor Mastery Rubric

| Level | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Emerging (1)** | Believes waves carry matter; cannot apply v=fλ; confuses transverse and longitudinal. |
| **Developing (2)** | Applies v=fλ with support; struggles predicting what changes (vs. stays constant) when a wave crosses media. |
| **Proficient (3)** | Independently solves wave-speed problems; correctly explains why frequency stays constant across media; predicts interference outcomes from diagrams; scores ≥80% on unit quiz. |
| **Advanced (4)** | Explains resonance and standing wave formation with real examples; connects wave concepts across sound and light; scores ≥90% on unit quiz. |

## Gradable Evidence Checklist

- [ ] Unit quiz score recorded (target ≥80%)
- [ ] At least 3 practice problems shown with full work
- [ ] Cornell notes cue-column self-quiz completed
- [ ] One real-world application explained in the student's own words
- [ ] PhET "Wave Interference" used to observe at least one constructive and one destructive interference pattern

## Suggested Exit Ticket (3 questions, 5 minutes)

1. A wave has a frequency of 10 Hz and travels at 20 m/s. Find its wavelength.
2. Explain why a cork bobbing on a water wave doesn't travel toward shore with the wave.
3. What's the difference between constructive and destructive interference?
