# Unit 8: Waves — SAT/ACT Practice

- **SAT Math** turns v=fλ into **direct substitution and inverse-proportion** problems.
- **ACT Science** turns waves into **data tables of frequency vs. wavelength** across different media, or interference-pattern diagrams to interpret.

## SAT Math Style

**Q1.** A wave has a frequency of 20 Hz and travels at 100 m/s. Find its wavelength.

<details><summary>Show Answer</summary>

**5 m.** λ=v/f=100/20=5 m.
</details>

---

**Q2.** If a wave's period is 0.02 s, what is its frequency?

<details><summary>Show Answer</summary>

**50 Hz.** f=1/T=1/0.02=50 Hz.
</details>

## ACT Science Reasoning Style

**Q3.** A student measures wave speed in different media, keeping the
source frequency fixed at 10 Hz:

| Medium | Wave speed (m/s) | Wavelength (m) |
|---|---|---|
| Air | 340 | 34 |
| Water | 1480 | 148 |
| Steel | 5960 | ? |

Based on the pattern, what is the missing wavelength in steel?

<details><summary>Show Answer</summary>

**596 m.**

λ=v/f=5960/10=596 m. This is a "complete the pattern using the underlying
formula" question — every row should satisfy v=fλ with the same f=10 Hz,
so you can either compute directly or notice λ=v/10 holds in every row.
</details>

---

**Q4.** Based on the table in Q3, does frequency change across the three
media?

<details><summary>Show Answer</summary>

**No — frequency stays fixed at 10 Hz in all three; only speed and wavelength change.**

This directly reinforces the most commonly missed concept in the unit —
students sometimes assume the table shows frequency varying because speed
and wavelength clearly do.
</details>

---

**Q5.** A diagram shows two circular wave patterns overlapping from two
point sources. At certain points, the waves reinforce each other (larger
amplitude); at others, they cancel. What is this overall pattern called?

<details><summary>Show Answer</summary>

**An interference pattern** (from the superposition of the two sources' waves — a mix of constructive and destructive interference regions).

This is the standard ACT Science "identify what a diagram is showing"
question — the answer doesn't require computation, just correctly naming
the phenomenon.
</details>

---

## Answer Key Summary

1. 5 m  2. 50 Hz  3. 596 m  4. No, frequency unchanged  5. Interference pattern
