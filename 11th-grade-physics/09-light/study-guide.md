# Light — Quick-Reference Study Guide

## Definitions

- **Electromagnetic spectrum** — full range of EM waves; visible light is a narrow band within it
- **Law of reflection** — angle of incidence = angle of reflection, both from the normal
- **Refraction** — bending of light crossing between media of different optical density
- **Index of refraction (n)** — n = c/v, measures how much a medium slows light
- **Real image** — light rays actually converge; can be projected on a screen
- **Virtual image** — light rays only appear to converge; cannot be projected

## Formulas

- Snell's Law: n₁sin(θ₁) = n₂sin(θ₂)
- Index of refraction: n = c/v

## Quick-Reference Table

| Situation | Key fact |
|---|---|
| Measuring any reflection/refraction angle | Always from the normal, never from the surface |
| Light traveling through vacuum | No medium needed — unlike sound |
| Object's apparent color | The wavelength(s) it reflects, not an inherent property |
| All colors in a vacuum | Travel at the same speed, c |
| Inside a medium (e.g. glass) | Different colors travel at (slightly) different speeds — causes prism dispersion |
| Converging lens / concave mirror | Can form real images |
| Flat mirror | Always forms a virtual image |

## Practice Problems

1. A ray hits a mirror at an angle of incidence of 35°. Find the angle of reflection.
2. Light goes from air (n=1.00) into water (n=1.33) at 40° incidence (sin40°≈0.64). Find the refraction angle.
3. A green shirt is viewed under pure red light. What color does it appear?
4. Does a movie theater projector form a real or virtual image on the screen?
5. What's the index of refraction of a medium where light travels at 2×10⁸ m/s? (c=3×10⁸ m/s)
6. Why does white light separate into colors passing through a prism, but not through a flat pane of glass at normal (90°) incidence?

<details><summary>Show Answers</summary>

1. 35° (equal to angle of incidence)
2. n₁sin(θ₁)=n₂sin(θ₂) → (1.00)(0.64)=(1.33)sin(θ₂) → sin(θ₂)≈0.481 → θ₂≈28.8°
3. Black/very dark (no green light available to reflect)
4. Real (projected onto the screen)
5. n=c/v=3×10⁸/2×10⁸=1.5
6. A prism's angled surfaces cause light to refract twice at non-zero angles, separating colors by their different bend amounts; a flat pane at normal incidence has essentially no bending to separate, so colors stay together
</details>
