# Kinematics — Quick-Reference Study Guide

## Definitions

- **Distance** — total path length traveled (scalar, always positive)
- **Displacement (Δx)** — straight-line change in position, with direction (vector)
- **Speed** — distance / time (scalar)
- **Velocity (v)** — displacement / time, with direction (vector)
- **Acceleration (a)** — rate of change of velocity; happens from speeding up, slowing down, *or* changing direction
- **Free fall** — motion under gravity alone; a = -g near Earth's surface (g ≈ 9.8 m/s²)

## Formulas

| Equation | Solves for (missing variable) |
|---|---|
| v = v₀ + at | Δx not needed |
| Δx = v₀t + ½at² | v not needed |
| v² = v₀² + 2aΔx | t not needed |
| Δx = ½(v₀ + v)t | a not needed |

**Sign convention:** pick one direction as positive at the start of a problem and stay consistent all the way through.

## Quick-Reference Table

| Quantity | Symbol | Units | Vector or Scalar |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distance | d | m | Scalar |
| Displacement | Δx | m | Vector |
| Speed | — | m/s | Scalar |
| Velocity | v | m/s | Vector |
| Acceleration | a | m/s² | Vector |
| Gravity (near Earth) | g | 9.8 m/s² | Constant magnitude, direction = down |

**Graph reading:** slope of x-t graph = velocity. Slope of v-t graph = acceleration. Area under v-t graph = displacement.

## Practice Problems

1. A skater accelerates from rest at 1.5 m/s² for 8 s. Find her displacement and final velocity.
2. A car traveling at 30 m/s brakes at -6 m/s². How far does it travel before stopping?
3. A ball is dropped from a 60 m tower. How long until it hits the ground, and what's its speed on impact?
4. A ball is thrown straight up at 20 m/s. How high does it rise, and how long is it in the air total (back to the same height)?
5. A cyclist travels at a constant 8 m/s for 5 s, then accelerates at 2 m/s² for the next 4 s. Find the total distance covered.
6. Two cars start 500 m apart, driving toward each other at 20 m/s and 30 m/s. How long until they meet?

<details><summary>Show Answers</summary>

1. Δx = ½(1.5)(8²) = 48 m; v = 0 + 1.5(8) = 12 m/s
2. v² = v₀² + 2aΔx → 0 = 900 + 2(-6)Δx → Δx = 75 m
3. Δx = ½gt² → 60 = ½(9.8)t² → t ≈ 3.5 s; v = gt ≈ 34.3 m/s
4. Rise: v²=v₀²-2gΔx → 0=400-19.6Δx → Δx≈20.4 m. Total time up+down: t=2v₀/g=2(20)/9.8≈4.08 s
5. Stage 1: 8×5=40 m. Stage 2: v₀t+½at²=8(4)+½(2)(16)=32+16=48 m. Total = 88 m
6. Closing speed = 50 m/s over 500 m → t = 10 s
</details>
