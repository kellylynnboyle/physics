# Grading & Course Policies

Pulled directly from the official course syllabus: **Physics 1A & 1B, Corona
Del Mar High School** (Instructor: Mr. Selby, pselby@nmusd.us).

## Course Description

Physics 1A & 1B provides lab experience, real-world connections, and a
theoretical framework across a full year of physics and earth science,
aligned to the NGSS (Next Generation Science Standards) for California.

- **Semester 1:** Kinematics, Projectile Motion, Newton's Laws, Momentum, Work & Energy
- **Semester 2:** Electricity, Magnetism, Waves, Light, Plate Tectonics, Earthquakes, Nuclear Physics

## Late Work Policy

- Classwork (homework, lab reports, quizzes) is **late if not submitted
  before the unit test.** Late work caps out at a **maximum score of 70%.**
- Zeros are placed in the gradebook as placeholders for missing/late work.
  Late work can replace a zero, **but the end of the quarter is the last
  date to earn partial credit** — check Schoology for exact cutoffs.
- **One test score per semester can be replaced by your Final Exam
  percentage**, if that helps your grade — this happens automatically at
  the end of the semester based on whichever helps you more.

## Attendance / Tardy Policy

Follows the Corona Del Mar High School Citizenship Rubric:
- **2nd tardy** → email to parents/coaches
- **4th tardy in a quarter** → email to parents/coaches, Citizenship grade
  drops to "Unsatisfactory" (U), and the student becomes **ineligible for
  extracurricular activities** (sports, performances, etc.) until resolved

## Academic Honesty

Any intentional use of another's ideas, words, or work as your own —
including misuse of published material or another student's work — is
plagiarism. **First offense: automatic zero on the assignment** plus a "U"
(Unsatisfactory) Citizenship grade for the quarter.

## Classroom Expectations

- Be in your seat, ready to learn, before the bell.
- Phones go in the "Parking Lot" unless the teacher says otherwise for the
  lesson.
- Check the whiteboard calendar and Schoology to keep your agenda updated.
- Reset your lab station (equipment + furniture) at the end of class.
- Bathroom breaks: use Break/Lunch when possible; after the main lesson,
  ask to be excused (phone stays in the Parking Lot).

## Office Hours

Monday (Intervention), Tuesday (lunch), Thursday (lunch) — other times by
appointment.

## Supplies

- Pencils (#2) — especially for calculations and tests
- Scientific calculator for daily use (TI-84, etc.) — loaner Casio
  fx-260solar calculators available; owning one costs roughly $10
- Binder for notes and assignments (recommended)
- 3–4 colored pencils (recommended, for clearer diagrams in notes)
