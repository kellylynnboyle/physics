# Newton's Laws — Quick-Reference Study Guide

## Definitions

- **Inertia** — an object's tendency to resist a change in its state of motion
- **Net force (Fnet)** — the vector sum of all forces acting on an object
- **Newton's First Law** — an object at rest stays at rest, and an object in motion stays in motion at constant velocity, unless acted on by a net external force
- **Newton's Second Law** — Fnet = ma
- **Newton's Third Law** — for every action force, there is an equal and opposite reaction force, acting on a *different* object
- **Normal force (N)** — support force perpendicular to a surface
- **Friction (f)** — force resisting relative motion between surfaces (static, before sliding; kinetic, during sliding)

## Formulas

- Fnet = ma
- Weight: W = mg
- Static friction (max): Ff = μₛN
- Kinetic friction: Ff = μₖN
- On a frictionless incline (angle θ): along-incline component of gravity = mg sin θ; normal force = mg cos θ

## Quick-Reference Table

| Situation | Key fact |
|---|---|
| Constant velocity (including at rest) | Fnet = 0 — does NOT mean zero forces, just zero *net* force |
| Object accelerating | Fnet points in the same direction as acceleration |
| Third Law pair | Always equal magnitude, opposite direction, acting on two *different* objects — never cancels on one object's FBD |
| Mass vs. weight | Mass (kg) is constant everywhere; weight (N) = mg, changes with local gravity |
| Objects in free fall | a = g regardless of mass (mass cancels in Fnet=ma when Fnet=mg) |

## Practice Problems

1. A 900 kg car accelerates from 0 to 20 m/s in 5 s. What net force was required?
2. A 50 kg skier is pulled up a 15° frictionless slope at constant velocity. Find the tension in the tow rope.
3. A 6 kg block is pushed with 30 N across a floor with 10 N of kinetic friction opposing it. Find its acceleration.
4. Two boxes, 3 kg and 9 kg, are each pushed by a 27 N force on frictionless ground. Compare their accelerations.
5. An 800 kg elevator accelerates upward at 1.5 m/s². Find the cable tension. (g = 9.8 m/s²)
6. A 4 kg block sits on a 25° incline with friction (μₖ = 0.2). Find its acceleration down the slope.

<details><summary>Show Answers</summary>

1. a=(20-0)/5=4 m/s². F=ma=900×4=3600 N
2. Constant velocity → Fnet=0 along slope → T = mg sin(15°) = 50×9.8×0.259 ≈ 127 N
3. Fnet=30-10=20 N. a=20/6≈3.33 m/s²
4. a₁=27/3=9 m/s²; a₂=27/9=3 m/s² — lighter box accelerates 3× faster (same force, inversely proportional to mass)
5. T - mg = ma → T = m(g+a) = 800(9.8+1.5) = 9040 N
6. Along-incline gravity component: g sin(25°)≈4.14 m/s². Normal force: mg cos(25°)≈35.5 N → friction=0.2×35.5≈7.1 N → friction accel = 7.1/4≈1.78 m/s² (opposing motion). Net a = 4.14-1.78≈2.36 m/s²
