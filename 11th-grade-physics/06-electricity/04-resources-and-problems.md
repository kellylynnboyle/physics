## Practice Problems

1. Two charges of +5 μC and -3 μC are separated by 0.2 m. Find the force between them (attractive or repulsive?).
2. A circuit has a 6 V battery and three resistors in series: 2 Ω, 3 Ω, 5 Ω. Find total current and the voltage drop across each resistor.
3. Three resistors (10 Ω, 10 Ω, 10 Ω) are connected in parallel to a 20 V source. Find total resistance and total current.
4. A toaster draws 8 A from a 120 V outlet. Find its resistance and power consumption.
5. A circuit has a 2 Ω resistor in series with a parallel combination of two 6 Ω resistors, all connected to a 10 V battery. Find the total resistance of the circuit.
6. An electric heater uses 1500 W and runs 3 hours a day for 30 days. At $0.15/kWh, what's the monthly cost?

## Real-World Applications

- **Home wiring:** household outlets are wired in parallel specifically so each appliance gets full voltage (120V) independently — if one device fails or is unplugged, others keep working.
- **Fuses/circuit breakers:** sized based on current (I=P/V) to prevent house fires from too much current flowing through wiring rated for less.
- **Electric bills:** utility companies bill in kWh — literally Power × time — making the P=E/t relationship directly visible on your family's monthly statement.
- **Battery-powered devices:** device runtime is a direct application of E=Pt — a device's power draw and battery capacity (in Wh or mAh) determine how long it lasts.
