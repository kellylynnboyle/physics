**Course:** 11th Grade Physics & Earth Science (NGSS-aligned, California)
**Standards reference:** [CDE NGSS for California](https://www.cde.ca.gov/pd/ca/sc/ngssstandards.asp)

This file is the master checklist. Instructors track pacing and assessment here; students track their own progress with the checkboxes.

| Weeks | Unit | Lab anchor |

## Learning objectives by unit (student-facing "I can" statements)

**Kinematics**
- I can distinguish distance from displacement and speed from velocity.
- I can calculate average and instantaneous velocity and acceleration from data or graphs.
- I can read and sketch position-time, velocity-time, and acceleration-time graphs and connect slope/area to physical quantities.
- I can apply the kinematic equations to solve 1D constant-acceleration and free-fall problems.

**Projectile Motion**
- I can explain why horizontal and vertical motion are independent.
- I can decompose an initial velocity into horizontal and vertical components.
- I can calculate time of flight, range, and maximum height for horizontal and angled launches.
- I can predict and explain a projectile's trajectory shape.

**Newton's Laws**
- I can state and apply Newton's first, second, and third laws to real scenarios.
- I can draw a correct free-body diagram for an object in equilibrium or accelerating.
- I can calculate net force, acceleration, and mass using F = ma.
- I can distinguish mass from weight and explain the role of friction and normal force.

**Momentum**
- I can calculate momentum and impulse and apply the impulse-momentum theorem.
- I can apply conservation of momentum to solve collision problems.
- I can distinguish elastic from inelastic collisions using energy and momentum.
- I can design and justify a device that reduces force during a collision (HS-PS2-3).

**Work & Energy**
- I can calculate work done by a force, including cases where force and displacement aren't parallel.
- I can calculate kinetic and gravitational potential energy and apply the work-energy theorem.
- I can apply conservation of mechanical energy to solve motion problems without kinematics.
- I can calculate power and explain energy transformations in a real system (HS-PS3-1, -2, -3).

## Assessment criteria (how mastery is measured)

| Level | Description |
|---|---|
| **Exceeds (4)** | Solves multi-step/novel problems, justifies reasoning with correct physics vocabulary, connects concept to a real-world system unprompted. |
| **Meets (3)** | Solves standard problems correctly, applies correct formulas/diagrams, explains reasoning when asked. |
| **Approaching (2)** | Solves problems with minor errors (sign, units, formula selection); needs prompting to explain reasoning. |
| **Beginning (1)** | Identifies relevant quantities but cannot yet execute the full solution; conceptual gaps present. |

Each unit is assessed with: (1) a self-check quiz (formative, unlimited retakes), (2) a hands-on lab with data analysis, (3) a written unit test (conceptual + numerical), and where noted, (4) an engineering design task scored against the rubric above.
