# Standards Alignment (California NGSS, HS Physics & Earth Science model)

Performance expectations (PEs) are the course learning goals. Wording below is paraphrased/abridged — confirm against
<https://www.cde.ca.gov/pd/ca/sc/ngssstandards.asp>.

| PE | Summary | Units that build it |
|---|---|---|
| **HS-PS2-1** | Analyze data to support the claim that Newton's second law describes the relationship among net force, mass, and acceleration. | 1 Kinematics, 2 Projectile, **3 Newton's Laws** |
| **HS-PS2-2** | Use mathematical representations to support the claim that total momentum of a system is conserved when there is no net external force. | **4 Momentum** |
| **HS-PS2-3** | Design, evaluate, and refine a device that minimizes the force on a macroscopic object during a collision. | **4 Momentum** (impulse), 5 Energy |
| **HS-PS3-1** | Create a computational model to calculate the change in energy of one component of a system when the change in energy of the other components and the energy flows are known. | **5 Work & Energy** |
| **HS-PS3-2** | Develop and use models to illustrate that energy at the macroscopic scale is the combination of energy associated with the motions of particles (objects) and with positions (relative position of objects/fields). | **5 Work & Energy** |
| **HS-PS3-3** | Design, build, and refine a device that works within given constraints to convert one form of energy into another form. | **5 Work & Energy** (capstone) |
| **HS-ETS1-2 / 3** | Break a complex problem into smaller problems; evaluate solutions against criteria and constraints. | Capstone design challenge |

## Science & Engineering Practices emphasized

Analyzing and interpreting data (graphs, motion sensors, video analysis) · Using mathematics and computational thinking ·
Developing and using models (free-body diagrams, energy bar charts, motion maps) · Planning and carrying out investigations ·
Constructing explanations and designing solutions (CER: Claim–Evidence–Reasoning).

## Crosscutting concepts emphasized

Cause and effect · Systems and system models · Energy and matter (conservation) · Patterns · Scale, proportion, and quantity.
