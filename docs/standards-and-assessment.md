# Standards Alignment, Objectives & Assessment

## NGSS (California) alignment
Source: California NGSS, High School Physics & Earth Science course model (https://www.cde.ca.gov/pd/ca/sc/ngssstandards.asp). Performance expectation (PE) wording below is paraphrased; confirm exact text on the CDE site before publishing to families.

| Unit | PEs addressed | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Kinematics | Supports HS-PS2-1 | Not a PE by itself; builds the math and graph skills the PEs assume. |
| 2. Projectile Motion | HS-PS2-1 | Predict 2-D motion with F = ma. |
| 3. Newton's Laws | HS-PS2-1, HS-PS2-4 | Forces, F = ma, gravitation and Coulomb's law (the latter extends in semester 2). |
| 4. Momentum | HS-PS2-2, HS-PS2-3 | Conservation of momentum; design a device that minimizes collision force. |
| 5. Work & Energy | HS-PS3-1, HS-PS3-2, HS-PS3-3 | Models, macroscopic energy, device design. HS-PS3-4 (thermal equilibrium) and HS-PS3-5 (fields) continue in semester 2. |

**Semester 2 (outside this project, for planning):** electricity and magnetism (HS-PS2-4, HS-PS2-5, HS-PS3-5), waves and light (HS-PS4-1 to PS4-5), plate tectonics and earthquakes (HS-ESS2-1, HS-ESS2-3), nuclear physics (HS-PS1-8).

**Science & Engineering Practices used:** Developing and using models · Planning and carrying out investigations · Analyzing and interpreting data · Using mathematics and computational thinking · Designing solutions.

## Per-unit workflow checklist
- [ ] Pre-assessment (5 questions, ungraded)
- [ ] Cornell notes completed (cues, notes, 3-sentence summary)
- [ ] Interactive quiz on the unit, scoring 7/9 or better (retry missed questions)
- [ ] Practice problems 1–5 done with units and diagrams
- [ ] Lab report or design project submitted
- [ ] Real-world application reflection (one paragraph)
- [ ] Unit test
- [ ] Revisit missed objectives (retake or correction form)

## Assessment weights (suggested; instructors may adjust)
| Component | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Quizzes & practice (formative) | 15% | Completion plus mastery retries |
| Cornell notes | 10% | Graded on the rubric below |
| Labs & design projects | 30% | Aligned to SEPs and PEs |
| Unit tests | 35% | Mix of conceptual, graph, and multi-step problems |
| Reflection / real-world task | 10% | |

## Rubric: Cornell notes (4 points each, 12 total)
| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cues/questions | Question for each idea, tests understanding | Questions mostly clear | Keywords only | Missing |
| Notes | Accurate, includes diagrams and worked example | Accurate, minor gaps | Several errors | Mostly missing |
| Summary | 3 sentences in own words with a connection | Accurate summary | Copied or vague | Missing |

## Rubric: lab / design project (16 points)
| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Question & prediction | Testable, with a quantitative prediction | Testable | Vague | Missing |
| Data & analysis | Multiple trials, graph, uncertainty | Graph and calculation | Raw data only | Incomplete |
| Claim–evidence–reasoning | Claim backed by data and physics law | Claim and evidence | Claim only | None |
| Evaluation / redesign | Sources of error and improvement quantified | Errors named | Generic | Missing |

## Instructor notes
- The interactive quiz saves scores in each student's own browser only. For class-wide data, have students screenshot the results screen or use a form.
- Suggested order and pacing: Kinematics (2 wk) → Projectile (1.5) → Newton (3) → Momentum (2.5) → Work & Energy (3) ≈ 12 weeks, leaving buffer for review and the semester exam.
- Worked answers on practice problems are rounded to 3 significant figures using g = 9.8 m/s².
