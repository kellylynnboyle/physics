# Assessment Criteria and Progress Tracking

## Mastery levels (used in every unit)
| Level | Name | Meaning | Typical quiz/test range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | Advanced | Applies ideas to new, multi-step, or design situations and evaluates results | 90 to 100% |
| 3 | Proficient | Meets the standard independently, with correct units, signs, and reasoning | 80 to 89% |
| 2 | Developing | Partial understanding; needs prompts; recurring errors | 60 to 79% |
| 1 | Beginning | Limited understanding; cannot yet complete tasks without support | below 60% |

**Mastery rule:** A unit is "mastered" when the student has (a) ≥ 80% on that topic's interactive quiz, (b) Proficient or higher on the unit test, and (c) a completed lab or design product. A student may retake the quiz unlimited times; the best score is saved in the browser.

## Evidence sources and suggested weights
| Evidence | Weight | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Unit test | 35% | Mix of conceptual, calculation, and graph items |
| Labs and design challenges | 25% | Use the lab rubric below |
| Quizzes (interactive) and practice sets | 15% | Completion plus mastery score |
| Cornell notes | 10% | Complete cues, summary, and IXL plan |
| Homework/IXL practice | 15% | SmartScore ≥ 80 on assigned skills |

Adjust the weights to your school's grading policy.

## Lab report rubric (4 points each, 20 total)
| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Question and prediction | Testable, with reasoning | Testable, brief reasoning | Vague | Missing |
| Data and graph | Accurate, labeled, units, fit line | Minor labeling errors | Incomplete | Missing |
| Calculations | Correct, with uncertainty | Correct, no uncertainty | Some errors | Missing |
| Claim-Evidence-Reasoning | Claim backed by data and physics law | Claim backed by data | Evidence unclear | Missing |
| Error analysis and improvement | Specific sources and fixes | General sources | Vague | Missing |

## Problem-solving rubric (for tests and practice)
| Element | Full credit expects |
|---|---|
| Diagram | Labeled sketch or free-body diagram |
| Known/unknown | List with units and signs |
| Equation | Correct equation written in symbols first |
| Substitution and answer | Correct arithmetic, units, sensible significant figures |
| Reasonableness | One sentence checking the answer |

## Using quiz results to reteach
The quiz review screen lists each missed question and its "common trap." Instructors can group missed items by misconception (see each unit's "Most-missed ideas") and plan a short mini-lesson or station for the top two.
