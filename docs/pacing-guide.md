# Pacing Guide: Semester 1 (about 18 weeks)

Suggested sequence for a block-schedule or daily class. Shorten or lengthen to your calendar.

| Weeks | Unit | Lab / activity | Assessment |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Course launch | Safety, measurement, significant figures, unit conversions, lab-notebook norms | Diagnostic (algebra, trig, graphing) |
| 2-4 | **1. Kinematics** | Constant-velocity buggy lab; cart on a ramp; PhET Moving Man | Quiz app (kinematics), unit assessment |
| 5-6 | **2. Projectile Motion** | Marble-launch landing-point prediction; PhET Projectile Motion | Quiz app (projectile), unit assessment |
| 7-10 | **3. Newton's Laws** | F vs. a cart lab (HS-PS2-1 data analysis); friction coefficient lab; elevator scale | Quiz app (Newton), unit assessment |
| 11-13 | **4. Momentum** | Cart collisions with motion sensors; egg-drop / crumple-zone design (HS-PS2-3) | Quiz app (momentum), unit assessment |
| 14-16 | **5. Work & Energy** | Energy of a rolling cart / pendulum; spring launcher; PhET Energy Skate Park; energy-conversion device (HS-PS3-3) | Quiz app (energy), unit assessment |
| 17 | Semester review | Capstone video analysis; "all topics" quiz mix | Capstone performance task |
| 18 | Final | | Semester exam |

## Weekly rhythm (suggested)

| Day | Focus |
|---|---|
| Mon | Introduce concept; start Cornell notes; demonstration or simulation |
| Tue | Worked examples; students fill cue column; practice problems |
| Wed | Lab or design activity |
| Thu | Data analysis / lab write-up; practice problems |
| Fri | Quiz-app attempt in class (10-15 min), review missed items, exit ticket on progress tracker |

## Spiral review

Use the "All topics" quiz on Fridays after Unit 2 so earlier content (for example, sign conventions in Unit 1) keeps coming back in Units 3-5.

## Bridge to Semester 2

Energy and momentum ideas feed directly into waves (energy transfer), electricity (work done moving charge, potential difference), and nuclear physics (mass-energy). Earth science (plate tectonics, earthquakes) revisits forces, wave propagation, and energy release.
