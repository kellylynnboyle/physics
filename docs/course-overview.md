This repository covers **Semester 1** in full. Semester 2 units are listed as a roadmap so the project can be extended.

### Note on kinematics and projectile motion
The NGSS performance expectations do not name kinematics or projectile motion as separate standards. They are the mathematical tools students need for HS-PS2-1 (analyzing data to support F = ma), so they are mapped to that standard as foundations. HS-PS2-1 is the standard that is *assessed*; kinematics and projectile motion are *prerequisite skills* for it.

## Three-dimensional learning
Each topic page lists the **Science and Engineering Practices** and **Crosscutting Concepts** that go with its standards. Across Semester 1, students should:
- **Analyze data** (lab graphs of x–t, v–t, a vs. F)
- **Use mathematics and computational thinking** (every unit)
- **Develop and use models** (free-body diagrams, energy bar charts, projectile trajectories)
- **Design solutions** (egg-drop collision device, energy-conversion device)
