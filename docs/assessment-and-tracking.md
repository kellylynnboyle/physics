# Assessment Criteria & Progress Tracking

## Grading structure (suggested weights; adjust to your department policy)
| Category | Weight | What it measures |
|---|---|---|
| Unit assessments (test + quiz) | 35% | Concepts and problem solving |
| Labs & design challenges | 25% | Practices: data, models, design (NGSS) |
| Cornell notes & practice (IXL, problem sets) | 20% | Preparation and independent practice |
| Formative checks (exit tickets, interactive quizzes) | 10% | Misconception monitoring |
| Reflection / error analysis | 10% | Metacognition |

## 4-point mastery scale (used on every unit)
| Score | Label | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Advanced | Applies to novel, multi-step or design situations; explains reasoning clearly |
| 3 | Proficient | Correct methods, units, signs and explanations on standard tasks (**goal: ≥ 80%**) |
| 2 | Developing | Right idea, with frequent errors or needs prompts |
| 1 | Beginning | Major misconceptions; needs reteaching |

## Lab / performance-task rubric (CER format)
| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Claim** | Precise, testable, tied to the standard | Clear and testable | Vague | Missing |
| **Evidence** | Well-organized data, graphs with units and uncertainty | Accurate data and graph | Incomplete or unlabeled | Absent |
| **Reasoning** | Links evidence to physics law with equations | Links evidence to law | Restates data | Missing |
| **Error analysis** | Quantifies sources and effects | Names two plausible sources | Names a vague source | None |
| **Communication** | Professional and concise | Clear | Hard to follow | Unreadable |

## Cornell notes rubric (10 points)
| Item | Points |
|---|---|
| Header: title, date, essential question | 1 |
| Cue column has real questions or keywords (not copied notes) | 3 |
| Notes include definitions, equations with units, and at least one labeled diagram or graph | 3 |
| Summary written in own words (2–3 sentences) | 2 |
| Neatness and organization | 1 |

## Interactive quiz policy
- The quizzes in `quizzes/index.html` use the questions students most often miss. They save the best score in the student's own browser (no accounts, no data leaves the device).
- **Proficient = 80% or higher.** Students may retake as often as needed; ask for a written error analysis for each missed question before a reset.
- Because scores are stored per browser, have students screenshot their results page to submit to the LMS if you need a record.
