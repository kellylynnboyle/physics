# Learning Objectives, Assessment and Progress Tracking

Semester 1 · Physics (11th grade) · Physics & Earth Science course model, California NGSS

> **Standards note:** Performance expectations below are the California NGSS high-school physical science standards (HS-PS) used for this course. Wording is paraphrased. Check the official text at <https://www.cde.ca.gov/pd/ca/sc/ngssstandards.asp> before publishing to families or aligning a district pacing guide.

## 1. Standards alignment (Semester 1)

| Unit | Primary NGSS performance expectation | What students do |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Kinematics | Supports HS-PS2-1 | Describe and predict motion mathematically and graphically |
| 2 Projectile Motion | Supports HS-PS2-1 | Predict 2-D motion by combining independent components |
| 3 Newton's Laws | **HS-PS2-1**, **HS-PS2-3**, HS-PS2-4 | Analyze data to support F = ma; design a device that minimizes force on an object; use gravitation |
| 4 Momentum | **HS-PS2-2**, **HS-PS2-3** | Use conservation of momentum for a system; evaluate a collision-safety design |
| 5 Work & Energy | **HS-PS3-1**, **HS-PS3-2**, **HS-PS3-3**, HS-PS3-4 | Build a computational energy model; relate energy to motion and position; design an energy-conversion device |

Kinematics and projectile motion are the mathematical foundation for HS-PS2-1 rather than standalone performance expectations.

**Practices emphasized in every unit:** analyzing and interpreting data, using mathematics and computational thinking, developing and using models, planning investigations, constructing explanations and designing solutions.
**Crosscutting concepts:** patterns, cause and effect, systems and system models, energy and matter, scale and proportion.

## 3. Assessment plan

| Category | Examples | Suggested weight |
|---|---|---|
| Formative checks | Cornell notes, interactive quizzes (`quiz/index.html`), exit tickets, whiteboard problems | 15% |
| Practice and homework | Practice sets in each topic file, IXL skills | 15% |
| Labs and design challenges | Lab reports, engineering design write-ups | 25% |
| Unit tests | Multiple choice + free response, drawn from the most-missed concepts | 35% |
| Semester project / cumulative exam | Cross-unit problem or model | 10% |

Adjust weights to match the department grading policy.

### Proficiency scale (applies to every objective)

| Level | Score | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4 Mastery | 90–100% | Solves novel, multi-step problems; explains reasoning in words, equations and graphs; spots misconceptions |
| 3 Proficient | 75–89% | Solves standard problems accurately; minor errors in signs, units or arithmetic |
| 2 Developing | 60–74% | Solves one-step problems with support; confusions between related concepts |
| 1 Needs review | < 60% | Cannot yet apply the concept; reteach and reassess |

Mastery goal for the quiz bank: **≥ 75% on first full attempt, or ≥ 90% after retakes of missed questions**.

### Lab report rubric (4 points each)

| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Question and hypothesis | Testable, tied to a physics model | Testable | Vague | Missing |
| Procedure and data | Reproducible, labelled, units, uncertainty | Complete with minor gaps | Incomplete | Missing |
| Analysis (graphs and calculations) | Correct, uses slope/area, compares to theory | Mostly correct | Several errors | Missing |
| Conclusion and error analysis | Claim-Evidence-Reasoning with specific sources of error | CER with general error | Claim only | Missing |
| Communication | Clear, organized, correct vocabulary | Mostly clear | Hard to follow | Missing |

### Engineering design rubric (HS-PS2-3, HS-PS3-3)

| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Criteria and constraints | Quantified and prioritized | Quantified | Qualitative | Missing |
| Physics model | Uses impulse or energy equations to justify the design | Qualitative physics link | Weak link | None |
| Testing and data | Multiple trials, graphs, iteration | Multiple trials | One trial | None |
| Evaluation and improvement | Evidence-based trade-offs and next steps | Reasonable next step | Opinion | Missing |

## 5. Suggested 18-week pacing

| Weeks | Unit | Milestones |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Kinematics | Moving Man, cart lab, quiz, test |
| 4–5 | Projectile Motion | Launch lab, quiz, test |
| 6–9 | Newton's Laws | FBD set, friction lab, force-minimizing design challenge, test |
| 10–12 | Momentum | Collision lab, crumple-zone challenge, test |
| 13–16 | Work & Energy | Skate-park bar charts, spring lab, energy-device project, test |
| 17–18 | Review and semester exam | Use the quiz "retry missed" mode |

Semester 2 (electricity, magnetism, waves, light, plate tectonics, earthquakes, nuclear physics) follows the same structure: add `topics/06-…`, new question banks in `quiz/questions.js`, and new rows in the tracker.
