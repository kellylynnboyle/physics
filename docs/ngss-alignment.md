# NGSS Alignment (California Physics & Earth Science Course Model)

Source for standards: California Department of Education NGSS page, <https://www.cde.ca.gov/pd/ca/sc/ngssstandards.asp>. Standard codes and wording below are paraphrased from the NGSS High School Physical Science performance expectations; **confirm the exact wording against the CDE site before publishing to families or including in a syllabus.**

## Semester 1 coverage

| Performance expectation | Short description | Units |
|---|---|---|
| **HS-PS2-1** | Analyze data to support the claim that Newton's second law describes the mathematical relationship among net force, mass and acceleration. | 1 Kinematics, 2 Projectile Motion, 3 Newton's Laws |
| **HS-PS2-2** | Use mathematical representations to support the claim that total momentum of a system is conserved when there is no net external force. | 4 Momentum |
| **HS-PS2-3** | Apply scientific and engineering ideas to design, evaluate and refine a device that minimizes the force on a macroscopic object during a collision. | 4 Momentum |
| **HS-PS2-4** | Use mathematical representations of Newton's law of gravitation and Coulomb's law to describe and predict forces. | 3 (gravitation); Coulomb's law in Semester 2 |
| **HS-PS3-1** | Create a computational model to calculate the change in energy of one component of a system when the change in energy of the other component(s) and the energy flows are known. | 5 Work & Energy |
| **HS-PS3-2** | Develop and use models to illustrate that energy at the macroscopic scale can be accounted for as a combination of energy associated with the motions of particles (objects) and energy associated with the relative positions of particles (objects). | 5 Work & Energy |
| **HS-PS3-3** | Design, build and refine a device that works within given constraints to convert one form of energy into another form of energy. | 5 Work & Energy |

## Practices and crosscutting concepts emphasized
- **Science & Engineering Practices:** Analyzing and Interpreting Data; Using Mathematics and Computational Thinking; Developing and Using Models; Planning and Carrying Out Investigations; Constructing Explanations and Designing Solutions
- **Crosscutting Concepts:** Cause and Effect; Systems and System Models; Energy and Matter (conservation); Patterns

## Mapping unit objectives to standards
| Unit | Objectives | Evidence of standard mastery |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Kinematics | K1–K5 | Graph/equation work, motion-sensor lab (data analysis for PS2-1) |
| 2 Projectile Motion | P1–P5 | Launch-prediction lab, simulation model |
| 3 Newton's Laws | N1–N6 | a-vs-F and a-vs-1/m lab with CER write-up |
| 4 Momentum | M1–M5 | Collision-cart data (PS2-2); design challenge (PS2-3) |
