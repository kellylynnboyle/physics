## Learning objectives (student-friendly "I can…" statements)

### Unit 1 – Kinematics
- K1. I can distinguish distance/displacement and speed/velocity.
- K2. I can calculate average velocity and acceleration.
- K3. I can read and sketch position–time and velocity–time graphs (slope and area meanings).
- K4. I can use the constant-acceleration equations to solve 1-D problems.
- K5. I can model free fall with a = 9.8 m/s² downward.

### Unit 2 – Projectile Motion
- PM1. I can explain why horizontal and vertical motion are independent.
- PM2. I can resolve an initial velocity into components.
- PM3. I can find time of flight, range, and max height for horizontal and angled launches.
- PM4. I can predict and test a landing point in a lab.

### Unit 3 – Newton's Laws
- N1. I can state and apply the three laws, including identifying action–reaction pairs.
- N2. I can draw free-body diagrams and find net force.
- N3. I can solve F_net = ma problems with friction, inclines, and normal force.
- N4. I can analyze data to show a ∝ F_net and a ∝ 1/m (HS-PS2-1).
- N5. I can distinguish mass from weight.

### Unit 4 – Momentum
- M1. I can calculate momentum and impulse and relate them (J = FΔt = Δp).
- M2. I can apply conservation of momentum to collisions and explosions (HS-PS2-2).
- M3. I can classify collisions as elastic, inelastic, or perfectly inelastic.
- M4. I can design/evaluate a device that reduces collision force (HS-PS2-3).

### Unit 5 – Work & Energy
- E1. I can calculate work, including the angle between force and displacement.
- E2. I can calculate kinetic, gravitational potential, and elastic potential energy.
- E3. I can apply conservation of energy and the work–energy theorem (HS-PS3-1, 3-2).
- E4. I can calculate power and efficiency.
- E5. I can design and refine a device that converts energy between forms (HS-PS3-3).

## Proficiency scale (used for every objective)

| Level | Meaning | Evidence |
|-------|---------|----------|
| 4 – Extending | Applies in new/multi-step contexts, explains reasoning | Novel problem or design task done correctly with explanation |
| 3 – Proficient | Solves standard problems accurately with correct units | ≥ 80% on quiz section; practice problems correct |
| 2 – Developing | Understands concepts, makes calculation/representation errors | 60–79% |
| 1 – Beginning | Needs support | < 60% |

Mastery goal: level 3 on every objective before the unit test; re-assess objectives at level 1–2.

## Unit assessment criteria (suggested weights)

| Component | Weight |
|-----------|--------|

| Lab report | 25% |
| Unit test | 45% |

### Lab report rubric (4 pts each)
Question & variables identified · Data table with units/uncertainty · Graph (labeled, best-fit) ·
Calculation using the unit's equations · Claim–evidence–reasoning conclusion · Sources of error.

### Problem-solving rubric (4 pts)
Given/unknown listed · Correct equation chosen · Algebra then substitution with units · Answer with units and sense check.
