# Assessment & Progress Tracking

## Mastery scale (use for every objective)

| Level | Meaning | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 4 Extending | Applies the idea in a new or multi-step situation and explains why | Correct, with reasoning, on unfamiliar problems |
| 3 Proficient | Solves standard problems correctly with units and a diagram | At least 80% on unit problems; quiz 80%+ |
| 2 Developing | Understands the concept, makes calculation or setup errors | 60-79% |
| 1 Beginning | Needs support to start | Below 60% |

Proficiency target: **Level 3 on every objective** before the unit test.

## Problem-solving rubric (for written work, 4 points)

| Pts | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 1 | Diagram or sketch with known/unknown values labeled |
| 1 | Correct equation chosen and stated before substituting |
| 1 | Correct algebra and arithmetic, **units on every step** |
| 1 | Answer checked for sense (sign, size, units) |

## Lab report rubric (10 points)
Question and hypothesis (1), data table with units and uncertainty (2), graph with labeled axes and best-fit line (2), analysis using the relevant equation (2), conclusion that uses data as evidence (2), sources of error and an improvement (1).

## Weighting suggestion
Quizzes and homework 20%, labs 25%, unit tests 40%, unit project 15%.
