## Key concepts

- A projectile has **only gravity** acting (air resistance ignored) → a_x = 0, a_y = −9.8 m/s²
- **Components:** v₀ₓ = v₀ cos θ, v₀ᵧ = v₀ sin θ
- **x:** x = v₀ₓ t (constant velocity)
- **y:** y = v₀ᵧ t − ½gt², vᵧ = v₀ᵧ − gt
- **Time links** the two directions — solve the one with the most information first
- **Same-height launch/landing:** T = 2v₀ᵧ/g · H = v₀ᵧ²/(2g) · R = v₀² sin(2θ)/g, max at 45°
- Complementary angles (e.g. 30° and 60°) give the same range

## Cornell notes

| Cue | Notes |
|-----|-------|
| What is a projectile? | Object moving under gravity alone after launch. Path is a parabola. |
| Independence of motion | x and y motions don't affect each other. Dropped ball and horizontally thrown ball hit the ground together. |
| Horizontal motion | Constant velocity: a_x = 0, so vₓ never changes. |
| Vertical motion | Free fall: a_y = −9.8 m/s². |
| At the peak | v_y = 0 but vₓ ≠ 0, so speed is **not** zero. a = 9.8 m/s² down. |
| Components | v₀ₓ = v₀cosθ, v₀ᵧ = v₀sinθ. Draw the triangle first. |
| Horizontal launch | v₀ᵧ = 0. Fall time from h = ½gt². Range = v₀ₓ · t. |
| Best angle for range | 45° (same launch & landing height). Complementary angles tie. |
| Strategy | 1) Diagram + axes 2) Components 3) Find t from y 4) Use t in x 5) Check units. |

## Practice problems

1. A ball rolls off a 1.25 m table at 3.0 m/s. Time in the air and landing distance? *(0.51 s; 1.5 m)*
2. A kicker launches a ball at 25 m/s, 40° above horizontal. Find components. *(19.2 m/s, 16.1 m/s)*
3. For problem 2: time of flight and range (same height). *(3.3 s; 63 m)*
4. A cliff diver runs off a 30 m cliff at 4 m/s. Impact speed? *(vᵧ = 24.2, vₓ = 4 → 24.5 m/s)*
5. Which launch angle, 30°, 45°, or 60°, gives the **longest** time of flight at the same speed? *(60°)*
6. Challenge: A cannon at 50 m/s must hit a target 200 m away at the same height. Find the two possible angles. *(R = v² sin2θ/g → sin2θ = 0.784 → θ ≈ 25.8° or 64.2°)*

## Real-world applications

- Sports: basketball free throws, long jump, football punts, golf
- Firefighting: arc of water from a hose to a window
- Engineering: artillery tables, sprinkler design, water fountains
- Space/engineering: ballistic trajectories are a stepping stone to orbits
