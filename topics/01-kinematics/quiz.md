# Kinematics: Most-Missed Questions
Take the [interactive version](../../site/index.html#kinematics) for instant feedback. The "Misconception" line names the trap each question targets.

### 1. A runner completes one full lap of a 400 m track in 80 s and ends where she started. What is her average velocity?
- A. 5 m/s
- B. 0 m/s
- C. 400 m/s
- D. 80 m/s

### 2. A ball is tossed straight up. At the very top of its path, what are its velocity and acceleration?
- A. v = 0, a = 0
- B. v = 0, a = 9.8 m/s² downward
- C. v = 9.8 m/s upward, a = 0
- D. v = 0, a = 9.8 m/s² upward

### 3. A car has a velocity of +20 m/s and an acceleration of −3 m/s². The car is…
- A. speeding up
- B. slowing down
- C. moving at constant speed
- D. moving backward

### 4. On a velocity–time graph, a horizontal line at v = +5 m/s means the object is…
- A. at rest
- B. speeding up at 5 m/s²
- C. moving at a constant 5 m/s
- D. 5 m from the origin

### 5. In a vacuum, a hammer and a feather are dropped from the same height. Which hits the ground first?
- A. The hammer
- B. The feather
- C. They land at the same time
- D. It depends on the height only

### 6. A car starts from rest with constant acceleration. If it travels 10 m in the first 2 s, how far has it traveled after 4 s?
- A. 20 m
- B. 30 m
- C. 40 m
- D. 80 m

### 7. A car speeds up from 0 to 27 m/s in 6.0 s. What is its average acceleration?
- A. 4.5 m/s²
- B. 162 m/s²
- C. 0.22 m/s²
- D. 27 m/s²

### 8. A v–t graph shows a constant velocity of 4 m/s for 5 s. What is the displacement during that time?
- A. 0.8 m
- B. 9 m
- C. 20 m
- D. 1.25 m

---

## Answer key
1. **B** — *Misconception: Confusing distance with displacement.* Average velocity = displacement / time. She finished where she began, so displacement is 0 and average velocity is 0. Her average speed is 400 m / 80 s = 5 m/s.
2. **B** — *Misconception: Zero velocity means zero acceleration.* Gravity acts the whole time, so a = 9.8 m/s² downward even at the top. Velocity is changing from up to down, passing through zero for an instant.
3. **B** — *Misconception: Negative acceleration always means slowing.* Velocity and acceleration have opposite signs, so the car is slowing down. (If both were negative, a negative acceleration would mean speeding up in the negative direction.)
4. **C** — *Misconception: Reading the graph as a picture of the path.* A flat v–t line means velocity is not changing: constant velocity, zero acceleration. It is not at rest, since rest would be v = 0.
5. **C** — *Misconception: Heavier objects fall faster.* Without air resistance, all objects have the same free-fall acceleration g, so they land together. (Apollo 15 demonstrated this on the Moon.)
6. **C** — *Misconception: Assuming distance is proportional to time.* Δx = ½at², so distance grows with t². Doubling the time quadruples the distance: 4 × 10 m = 40 m.
7. **A** — *Misconception: Multiplying instead of dividing.* a = Δv / Δt = 27 / 6.0 = 4.5 m/s². Acceleration is a rate, so divide by time.
8. **C** — *Misconception: Not knowing that area under v–t = displacement.* Area of the rectangle = 4 m/s × 5 s = 20 m.
