# Work & Energy: Most-Missed Questions
Take the [interactive version](../../site/index.html#energy) for instant feedback. The "Misconception" line names the trap each question targets.

### 1. You carry a heavy box horizontally at constant speed across a room. How much work does the upward force of your arms do on the box?
- A. Equal to its weight times the distance
- B. Zero
- C. Equal to its kinetic energy
- D. Negative

### 2. A car doubles its speed from 15 m/s to 30 m/s. Its kinetic energy…
- A. doubles
- B. triples
- C. quadruples
- D. stays the same

### 3. Two frictionless ramps have the same height but different lengths. A block slides from rest down each. Compare the speeds at the bottom.
- A. Faster on the steeper (shorter) ramp
- B. Faster on the longer ramp
- C. The speeds are equal
- D. Cannot be determined

### 4. Two students lift identical boxes to the same shelf. Student A takes 2 s; Student B takes 4 s. Which statement is correct?
- A. A does more work
- B. B does more work
- C. Same work, but A has more power
- D. Same work and same power

### 5. A sliding block comes to rest because of friction. What happened to its kinetic energy?
- A. It was destroyed
- B. It became thermal energy
- C. It became gravitational PE
- D. It was stored in the block's mass

### 6. A spring is compressed 2 cm and stores 1 J. How much energy is stored if it is compressed 4 cm?
- A. 2 J
- B. 4 J
- C. 8 J
- D. 1 J

### 7. A car going 20 m/s skids 40 m to a stop on dry pavement. At 40 m/s (same road), the skid distance is about…
- A. 80 m
- B. 120 m
- C. 160 m
- D. 40 m

### 8. A 2.0 kg ball is thrown straight up at 10 m/s. Ignoring air resistance, what maximum height does it reach?
- A. 10 m
- B. 5.1 m
- C. 20 m
- D. 2.5 m

---

## Answer key
1. **B** — *Misconception: Effort equals work.* W = Fd cos θ. The lifting force is perpendicular to the displacement (θ = 90°), so cos θ = 0 and W = 0. You may feel tired, but physically no work is done on the box.
2. **C** — *Misconception: KE is proportional to speed.* KE = ½mv² grows with v². Doubling v multiplies KE by 4.
3. **C** — *Misconception: Path or steepness matters for final speed.* Energy conservation: mgh = ½mv² → v = √(2gh). It depends only on height, not on the path. (The time taken is different.)
4. **C** — *Misconception: Confusing work and power.* Both do W = mgh. Power = W/t, so the one who takes less time delivers twice the power.
5. **B** — *Misconception: Energy is lost or used up.* Energy is conserved. Friction converts kinetic energy into thermal energy of the block and surface.
6. **B** — *Misconception: Linear instead of quadratic relationship.* PE = ½kx². Doubling x quadruples the stored energy: 4 J.
7. **C** — *Misconception: Stopping distance is proportional to speed.* Friction does work −fd = −KE_i. KE quadruples when v doubles, so the distance quadruples: 160 m.
8. **B** — *Misconception: Forgetting to cancel mass.* ½mv² = mgh → h = v²/(2g) = 100/19.6 ≈ 5.1 m. Mass cancels.
