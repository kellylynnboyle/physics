# 1. Kinematics

**NGSS link:** Foundation for HS-PS2-1 (analyze data to support F = ma). Also practices *Analyzing and Interpreting Data* and *Using Mathematics*.
**Suggested pacing:** 2 weeks · **Lab:** Motion on a ramp with a motion sensor / video analysis

## Learning objectives (students can…)
- [ ] K1. Distinguish distance, displacement, speed, velocity, and acceleration, and say which are vectors.
- [ ] K2. Read and sketch position–time and velocity–time graphs; find velocity from slope and displacement from area.
- [ ] K3. Apply the constant-acceleration equations to solve one-dimensional problems.
- [ ] K4. Model free fall with a = g = 9.8 m/s² and explain why mass does not matter without air resistance.
- [ ] K5. Use sign conventions correctly to decide whether an object is speeding up or slowing down.

## Key concepts
| Concept | Meaning | Formula / unit |
|---|---|---|
| Position, displacement | Where you are; change in position (vector) | Δx = x_f − x_i (m) |
| Velocity | Rate of change of position (vector) | v = Δx/Δt (m/s) |
| Acceleration | Rate of change of velocity (vector) | a = Δv/Δt (m/s²) |
| Constant-a equations | Five variables: Δx, v₀, v, a, t | v = v₀ + at · Δx = v₀t + ½at² · v² = v₀² + 2aΔx · Δx = ½(v₀ + v)t |
| Free fall | Only gravity acts | a = −9.8 m/s² (up positive) |

## Cornell notes
| Cues / questions | Notes |
|---|---|
| What is the difference between distance and displacement? | Distance = total path length (scalar). Displacement = straight-line change in position with direction (vector). A round trip has displacement 0. |
| Speed vs. velocity? | Speed = distance/time (scalar). Velocity = displacement/time (vector). |
| What does acceleration mean? | Any change in velocity: speeding up, slowing down, **or turning**. a = Δv/Δt. |
| How do I read a graph? | x–t: slope = velocity. v–t: slope = acceleration, area = displacement. Horizontal on x–t = at rest. Horizontal on v–t = constant velocity. |
| When is an object speeding up? | When v and a have the **same sign**. Opposite signs mean slowing down. |
| How do I pick an equation? | List the knowns (Δx, v₀, v, a, t), name the unknown, choose the equation that has exactly those four variables. |
| Free fall facts | a = g everywhere on the path, including the peak. Time up = time down. Mass is irrelevant without air resistance. |

**Summary (students write, 3 sentences):** _Kinematics describes motion using displacement, velocity, and acceleration. Graph slopes and areas link them together. With constant acceleration, four equations let me solve any 1-D problem if I list my knowns first._

## Practice problems
1. A cyclist goes from 4 m/s to 10 m/s in 3 s. Find a and the distance covered. *(2 m/s², 21 m)*
2. A car brakes from 25 m/s to rest in 5 s. Find a and the stopping distance. *(−5 m/s², 62.5 m)*
3. A stone is dropped from a bridge and splashes 2.5 s later. How high is the bridge, and how fast is the stone going? *(≈30.6 m, 24.5 m/s)*
4. A ball is thrown up at 15 m/s. Find the time to the peak, the peak height, and total air time. *(1.53 s, 11.5 m, 3.06 s)*
5. Sketch the v–t graph for a ball thrown up and caught at the same height. What is the area under the whole graph? *(Straight line with slope −9.8; area 0)*
6. **Challenge:** A train at 30 m/s sees a stopped train 100 m ahead and brakes at 5 m/s². Does it stop in time? *(Yes: stopping distance v²/2a = 90 m < 100 m, leaving a 10 m gap.)*
