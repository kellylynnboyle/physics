# Unit 1 – Kinematics

NGSS: HS-PS2-1 (foundation) · Objectives K1–K5 (see [standards-and-assessment.md](../standards-and-assessment.md))

## Key concepts
- Scalar vs vector: distance/speed vs displacement/velocity
- Average velocity v = Δx/Δt; acceleration a = Δv/Δt
- Position–time and velocity–time graphs (slope, area)
- Constant-acceleration equations
- Free fall (g = 9.8 m/s² down)

## Cornell notes

| Cue / Question | Notes |
|---|---|
| What is the difference between distance and displacement? | **Distance**: total path length (scalar). **Displacement**: straight-line change in position with direction (vector). Walk 3 m east then 3 m west: distance 6 m, displacement 0. |
| What is the difference between speed and velocity? | **Speed** = distance/time (scalar). **Velocity** = displacement/time (vector). Average velocity v = Δx/Δt, units m/s. |
| How do I calculate acceleration, and what does a negative sign mean? | a = (v_f − v_i)/Δt, units m/s². Sign shows direction only. Speeding up when a and v point the **same** way; slowing down when **opposite**. Ex: 0 → 20 m/s in 5 s: a = 20/5 = 4 m/s². |
| What do slope and area mean on motion graphs? | x–t graph: slope = velocity. v–t graph: slope = acceleration, **area = displacement**. Flat line on x–t = at rest; flat line on v–t = constant velocity. |
| Which four equations apply when a is constant? | v = v₀ + at · Δx = v₀t + ½at² · v² = v₀² + 2aΔx · Δx = ½(v₀ + v)t. Pick the one that omits the variable you don't have or need. |
| How do I solve a motion problem? | List knowns/unknown with signs → choose equation → algebra first → substitute with units → check sense. Ex: 0 → 25 m/s at 5 m/s²: v² = 2ad → d = 625/10 = 62.5 m. |
| What is true of free fall? | Only gravity acts; a = 9.8 m/s² **down** at every point, including the top of a throw (where v = 0 momentarily). Dropped from rest 2 s: d = ½(9.8)(4) = 19.6 m. Mass doesn't change fall rate (no air). |

**Memory tricks:** "GUESS" (Given, Unknown, Equation, Substitute, Solve) · slope of x–t = v, slope of v–t = a · area under v–t = displacement.

**Summary:** Kinematics describes motion without asking why. Position, velocity and acceleration are linked by slopes
and areas on graphs and by four equations valid when acceleration is constant. Signs carry direction.

## Practice problems (g = 9.8 m/s²)
1. A cyclist covers 120 m in 15 s at constant speed. Find the speed. **Answer: 8 m/s**
2. A car accelerates from rest to 25 m/s at 5 m/s². How far does it travel? **62.5 m**
3. A ball is thrown straight up at 14.7 m/s. Find the time to reach the top and the max height. **1.5 s; 11.0 m** (h = v²/2g = 216.09/19.6)
4. Sketch the v–t graph for a car that speeds up, cruises, then brakes to a stop. Mark where displacement is the area.
