- **Practice tool:** Build a component table (v_x0, v_y0, t, x, y) for each new problem before solving — this prevents sin/cos mix-ups

## Practice Problems

1. A soccer ball is kicked at 18 m/s at 35° above horizontal. Find its range, max height, and total flight time.
2. A stunt car drives off a 25 m cliff horizontally at 22 m/s. How far from the base of the cliff does it land?
3. Show mathematically why 30° and 60° launches (same speed) produce equal ranges but different max heights.
4. An arrow is shot at 40 m/s. At what angle should it be launched to land 120 m away on level ground?
5. Find the velocity vector (magnitude and direction) of a projectile just before it lands, given its launch speed and angle.
6. A basketball player releases a shot; graph v_x and v_y separately vs. time for the whole flight.

## Real-World Applications

- **Sports:** Optimal launch angles in shot put, basketball free throws, and long jump
- **Ballistics & artillery:** Range tables historically used in military targeting
- **Water fountains & sprinklers:** Nozzle angle determines spray pattern and distance
- **Space launches (partial analogy):** Early-stage rocket trajectory planning before orbital mechanics takes over
- **Firefighting:** Aiming hose streams to clear obstacles and reach a target distance
