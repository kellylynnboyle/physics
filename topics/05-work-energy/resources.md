## Practice Problems

1. A 60 kg skateboarder rolls down a frictionless 5 m ramp. Find their speed at the bottom using energy conservation.
2. A 2 kg block is pushed 3 m across a floor with 15 N of force at a 30° angle above horizontal. Find the work done by the applied force.
3. A spring (k = 200 N/m) is compressed 0.15 m. Find the elastic potential energy stored, then find the block's launch speed if it has mass 0.5 kg.
4. An elevator motor lifts a 300 kg elevator 20 m in 25 seconds at constant speed. Find the power output of the motor (ignore friction).
5. A 1000 kg roller coaster car has 40% of its mechanical energy lost to friction going down a hill of height 30 m. Find its actual speed at the bottom.
6. Compare the kinetic energy of a 1000 kg car at 20 m/s to a 2000 kg truck at 10 m/s — same momentum, different KE. Show the math.

## Real-World Applications

- **Hydroelectric dams:** Convert gravitational PE of stored water into electrical energy
- **Roller coasters:** Classic real-world demonstration of KE ⇌ PE conversion, minimized friction (near-conservation)
- **Car engines & horsepower ratings:** Power output comparisons between vehicles
- **Energy efficiency ratings:** Appliances (refrigerators, light bulbs) rated by how much input energy becomes useful output vs. waste heat
- **Archery & pole vaulting:** Elastic potential energy (bow, pole) converts into kinetic and gravitational PE
