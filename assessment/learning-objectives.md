# Learning Objectives and Assessment Criteria

Semester 1 · Physics (grade 11) · California NGSS, Physics and Earth Science course model

This page lists every objective, the standard it supports, and how mastery is judged. Students track their own progress in the [progress tracker](progress-tracker.md).

## How mastery is judged

Each objective is scored on the same four-level scale, from any piece of evidence (quiz, problem set, lab, project, or conversation).

| Level | Name | What it looks like |
|-------|------|--------------------|
| 4 | Extending | Solves unfamiliar problems, explains the reasoning, and can teach it to a classmate |
| 3 | **Proficient (mastery)** | Solves standard problems correctly with method shown; explains the idea in their own words |
| 2 | Developing | Gets the idea with notes or hints; makes errors in set-up, signs, or units |
| 1 | Beginning | Cannot yet start the problem or holds the common misconception |

**Unit mastery** means all of the following:

- [ ] Level 3 or higher on every objective in the unit
- [ ] 80% or better on the unit's most-missed quiz (7 of 8)
- [ ] Practice-problem target met (listed in each unit outline)
- [ ] Lab report or project at "Proficient" on the rubric below
- [ ] Cornell notes self-test: every cue answered aloud with the notes covered

Students below mastery restudy the objectives they missed (the quiz names them), then retake. Retakes are expected, not penalized.

## Lab report rubric

| Criterion | 4 Extending | 3 Proficient | 2 Developing | 1 Beginning |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|--------------|-------------|
| Question and variables | Question, IV, DV, and controls stated; explains why each control matters | Question, IV, DV, and controls stated | Variables named but mixed up or incomplete | No clear question or variables |
| Data | Organized table with units, repeated trials, uncertainty noted | Organized table with units and repeated trials | Table missing units or trials | Data missing or unreadable |
| Graph and analysis | Correct graph, best-fit line, slope with units, slope compared with theory | Correct graph, best-fit line, slope with units | Graph has axis or scale errors; slope missing or wrong | No graph |
| Claim, evidence, reasoning | Claim supported by data and physics principle; limits of the data discussed | Claim supported by data and tied to the physics principle | Claim stated with weak or missing evidence | No claim |
| Error analysis | Names specific error sources, their likely effect, and a fix | Names specific error sources and their likely effect | Lists "human error" only | None |

## Engineering design rubric

Used for the collision-safety device (HS-PS2-3) and the energy-conversion device (HS-PS3-3).

| Criterion | 4 Extending | 3 Proficient | 2 Developing | 1 Beginning |
|-----------|-------------|--------------|--------------|-------------|
| Problem and constraints | Criteria and constraints stated and ranked by priority | Criteria and constraints clearly stated | Some criteria or constraints missing | Not stated |
| Physics reasoning | Design choices justified with equations and estimates (FΔt = Δp, or energy in and out) | Design choices explained with the correct physics idea | Physics idea named but not connected to the design | No physics reasoning |
| Testing and data | Several trials with measurements, recorded in a table | At least two trials with measurements recorded | One trial or observations only | Not tested |
| Refinement | At least two revisions, each changing one feature based on test data, with results compared | One revision based on test data | Revision made without using data | No revision |
| Communication | Clear report or presentation including trade-offs and next steps | Clear report or presentation of design, data, and results | Report incomplete | No report |
