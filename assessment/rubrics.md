# Assessment Criteria & Rubrics

## Mastery levels (all units)
| Level | Score | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 4 – Mastery | 90–100% | Solves unfamiliar multi-step problems, explains reasoning in words/diagrams, spots and corrects errors |
| 3 – Proficient | 80–89% | Solves standard problems accurately; minor sign/unit errors |
| 2 – Developing | 65–79% | Partial setup; needs hints on concepts |
| 1 – Beginning | < 65% | Misconceptions persist; reteach and re-quiz |

**Unit "done" when:** all objectives checked, ≥ 80% on the quiz twice (different days), practice set complete, lab report ≥ 3.

## Problem-solving rubric (4 pts)
| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Diagram / representation (FBD, graph, motion map) | missing | incomplete | correct | correct, labeled, supports solution |
| Knowns / unknowns & equation choice | none | equation guessed | appropriate | justified |
| Math & units | many errors | some errors | correct | correct + sig figs |
| Sense check | none | said "ok" | compared to expectation | explains why plausible |

## Lab report rubric (4 pts)
Question & variables · Procedure/data table · Graph with best-fit & slope meaning · Claim–Evidence–Reasoning (CER) tied to the NGSS PE · Error analysis.

## Design-challenge rubric (HS-PS2-3 and HS-PS3-3)
| Criterion | 4-pt descriptor |
|---|---|
| Criteria & constraints | Clearly stated measurable goals (e.g., peak force, % efficiency) |
| Testing | ≥ 3 trials, data tabulated |
| Physics reasoning | Uses impulse or energy-conversion concepts quantitatively |
| Iteration | Documented change after test 1 with improvement shown |

## Suggested grade weighting (adjust locally)
Quizzes/tests 40% · Labs & design 25% · Practice sets 15% · Notes (Cornell) 10% · Participation/reflection 10%

## Most-missed concepts to watch for
Kinematics: a at the top of flight; speed vs velocity · Projectiles: vₓ at the peak; independence · Newton: normal force ≠ weight pair; "force of motion" · Momentum: sign and Δp for bounces · Energy: work = 0 when force ⊥ motion; KE ∝ v².
