# Assessment Rubrics

## Objective proficiency scale

Used for every learning objective in the [progress tracker](progress-tracker.md).

| Score | Level | What it looks like |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Extending | Applies the idea correctly to an unfamiliar situation and explains the reasoning, including why a common wrong answer is wrong |
| 3 | Proficient | Solves standard problems correctly with units, diagrams, and a sense check, without help |
| 2 | Developing | Sets up the problem correctly but makes errors in execution, or succeeds only with notes or prompting |
| 1 | Beginning | Cannot yet set up the problem; shows a misconception addressed in the unit quiz |

A score of 3 on every objective in a unit is the target. Students may reassess any objective after correcting the original work and completing the matching practice problems.

## Problem-solving rubric (problem sets and tests)

| Criterion | 2 points | 1 point | 0 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setup | Knowns and unknown listed with units; diagram where it helps | List or diagram incomplete | Missing |
| Physics | Correct principle and equation chosen | Correct principle, wrong form of the equation | Wrong principle |
| Math | Correct substitution and algebra | Minor arithmetic slip | Major errors |
| Answer | Correct value, units, and direction or sign | Value correct, but units or direction missing | Incorrect |
| Sense check | States why the answer is reasonable | Attempted | Missing |

## Lab report rubric (claim, evidence, reasoning)

| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Question and variables | Testable question; independent, dependent, and controlled variables all named | One variable type missing | Variables confused | Missing |
| Data | Organized table with units and repeated trials | Table with units, single trials | Units missing | Incomplete |
| Graph | Correct axes, labels, units, scale, and best-fit line | One element missing | Several elements missing | No usable graph |
| Claim | Answers the question directly | Partly answers | Unclear | Missing |
| Evidence | Cites specific values, slope, or area from the data | Cites data in general terms | Little data cited | None |
| Reasoning | Connects evidence to the physics principle; discusses error sources and their effect | Connects to the principle | Restates the claim | Missing |

## Engineering design rubric (HS-PS2-3, HS-PS3-3)

| Criterion | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Criteria and constraints | Listed and quantified | Listed | Partial | Missing |
| Design and physics | Design choices explained with impulse or energy ideas and calculations | Explained with the correct ideas | Loosely connected | Not connected |
| Testing | Multiple trials with measured results | One measured trial | Observations only | Not tested |
| Refinement | At least one revision made from test data, with before and after results | Revision made, results described | Revision proposed only | None |
| Communication | Clear report or presentation with diagrams and trade-offs | Clear, missing trade-offs | Hard to follow | Incomplete |

## Grading weights (suggested)

| Category | Weight |
|---|---|
| Unit tests | 40% |
| Labs and design projects | 30% |
| Quizzes (best score counts) | 15% |
| Cornell notes and practice problems | 15% |
