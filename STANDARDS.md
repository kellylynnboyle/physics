# Standards Alignment (CA NGSS, High School Physical Science)

| Code | Performance expectation (paraphrased) | Units |
|---|---|---|
| HS-PS2-1 | Analyze data to support the claim that Newton's second law describes the mathematical relationship among net force, mass, and acceleration. | 1, 2, 3 |
| HS-PS2-2 | Use mathematical representations to support the claim that total momentum of a system is conserved when there is no net external force. | 4 |
| HS-PS2-3 | Apply science and engineering ideas to design a device that minimizes the force on a macroscopic object during a collision. | 4 |
| HS-PS3-1 | Create a computational model to calculate the change in energy of one component of a system when the change in energy of the other components and the energy flows are known. | 5 |
| HS-PS3-2 | Develop and use models to illustrate that energy at the macroscopic scale can be accounted for as motion of particles/objects or energy stored in fields (position). | 5 |
| HS-PS3-3 | Design, build, and refine a device that converts one form of energy into another within given constraints. | 5 |
| HS-PS3-5 (Sem. 2 preview) | Model the forces and changes in energy between two charged plates / magnets. | — |

Science & Engineering Practices used throughout: analyzing data, using mathematics, developing models, constructing explanations, designing solutions.
Crosscutting concepts: cause and effect; systems and system models; energy and matter; patterns.
Math connections (CA CCSS): HS-N-Q (units), HS-A-CED (equations), HS-F-IF (graphs), HS-G-SRT (trigonometry for vectors).
