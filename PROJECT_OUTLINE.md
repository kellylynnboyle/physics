# 11th Grade Physics — Semester 1 Learning Project

**Course:** Physics & Earth Science (CA NGSS course model) — lab experience, real-world connections, and theoretical framework across a two-semester sequence.

**This project covers Semester 1:** Kinematics → Projectile Motion → Newton's Laws → Momentum → Work & Energy.
**Semester 2** (not covered in this project): electricity, magnetism, waves, light, plate tectonics, earthquakes, nuclear physics.

**Standards reference:** California NGSS (Next Generation Science Standards) — https://www.cde.ca.gov/pd/ca/sc/ngssstandards.asp. Standards are the learning goals for this course.

## How This Project Is Organized

Each unit folder contains:
1. **`notes.md`** — Cornell-style notes: cue questions, detailed notes, a summary, vocabulary, worked practice problems, real-world applications, learning objectives, and assessment criteria.
2. **`quiz.html`** — a self-contained, interactive quiz (open directly in any browser, no install needed) covering the 10 questions students most often get wrong on that topic, with instant feedback and explanations for every choice.

## Semester 1 Learning Objectives (Consolidated)

| Unit | Core Objectives |
|---|---|
| Kinematics | Distinguish distance/displacement and speed/velocity; solve the four kinematic equations; interpret motion graphs; analyze free fall |
| Projectile Motion | Decompose velocity into components; solve for time of flight, height, and range; explain independence of horizontal/vertical motion |
| Newton's Laws | State and apply all three laws; draw free-body diagrams; calculate net force and acceleration; distinguish mass/weight and static/kinetic friction |
| Momentum | Calculate momentum and impulse; apply the impulse-momentum theorem; apply conservation of momentum to elastic/inelastic collisions |
| Work & Energy | Calculate work, KE, and PE; apply the work-energy theorem; apply conservation of mechanical energy; distinguish work/energy/power |

Full DOK-leveled objectives and rubric-style assessment criteria are listed in each unit's `notes.md`.

## Suggested Pacing (10-Week Sequence)

| Week | Unit | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Kinematics | Motion graphs, kinematic equations, free fall |
| 3 | Projectile Motion | Vector components, time/height/range |
| 4–6 | Newton's Laws | FBDs, F=ma, friction, inclines |
| 7–8 | Momentum | Impulse, conservation, collision types |
| 9–10 | Work & Energy | Work-energy theorem, conservation of mechanical energy, semester review |

Adjust pacing to your school calendar and lab availability; each unit's notes list the specific lab suggested for that topic.
